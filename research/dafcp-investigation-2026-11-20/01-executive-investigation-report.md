# DAFCP™ investigation: executive report

**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Package index:** [00-README.md](00-README.md) · **Evidence:** [`evidence-register.csv`](../../data/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/evidence-register.csv); IDs DE001–[DE073] are cited inline.

**Scope:** public sources only, with no allegation of misconduct. A commercial or professional tie is reported as a tie. It is not treated as proof of influence over content.

---

## 1. Bottom line

1. **What DAFCP is.** It is a low-barrier, knowledge-tested designation:
   - no prerequisite;
   - about 30 hours across six online modules;
   - a proctored Pearson VUE exam, passed at a 70% course grade [DE017];
   - $1,495, or $1,195 for nonprofit staff [DE018];
   - recertification fees once earned [DE017, DE018, DE013].

   It issues from an accredited, insurer-governed, tuition-funded nonprofit that has run five straight years of operating deficits [DE003–DE010]. It is the only DAF-specific designation we found [DE068].

2. **Who built it, and with whom.** It is the online, scaled successor to a 2025 in-person certificate. The College says it developed that certificate "in collaboration with four premier sponsors: Fidelity Charitable, National Philanthropic Trust, Vanguard Charitable, and DAFgiving360" ([DE020], re-read by the lead).
   - The 2026 DAFCP names no partner [DE016].
   - The same four sponsors are the Premier Partners of the College's inaugural March 2027 DAF Forum. The Forum says "every general session maps to one of the six sections of the DAFCP curriculum" [DE037–DE039].
   - **Sponsor involvement is documented in the programme's lineage and in its companion event. It is not disclosed for the DAFCP content itself.**

3. **Who teaches it.** Using employer-published pages, 10 of the 22 people named on the DAFCP page currently work for a DAF sponsor or a sponsor's administrator. Counting former sponsor executives, the figure is 14 of 22 [DE033].
   - The largest cluster is **six people at Charitable Solutions LLC, including the programme director** ([DE026, DE027], lead re-check).
   - Charitable Solutions provides the day-to-day operations of Dechomai Foundation, a national noncash DAF sponsor. Dechomai paid the firm $4.43m for administrative services in TY2024 [DE028, DE029].
   - Current executives of NPT and DAFgiving360 are also on the list [DE031, DE032].
   - The DAFCP page prints no employers [DE019]. The College did disclose the programme director's Charitable Solutions role when it hired him in 2025 [DE027].

4. **What it teaches.** The public outline covers DAF law, tax and complex-asset mechanics: real estate, business interests, prearranged sale, UBTI, Notice 2017-73 and international grantmaking [DE044, DE045].
   - It omits the items an adviser needs to compare sponsors: payout distribution, fees and adviser-managed DAF pay, transfers between sponsors, inactivity policies and anonymity [DE046].
   - The programme director himself calls payout, transparency and inactive accounts "legitimate policy debates" [DE047].
   - **The curriculum is strongest where its faculty work (noncash gifts) and silent where sponsors compete (price, payout, portability).**

5. **Technology and AI.** The only technology content is a single bullet, "DAF technology tools" [DE057].
   - AI appears nowhere in the College's DAF materials [DE057–DE059].
   - No DAF technology vendor is named as a partner or speaker [DE040, DE058].
   - DAFCP prepares people to **operate within existing sponsor systems, not to evaluate or redesign them.** That is an inference from public text (§5).

6. **Adoption and reception.**
   - **Adoption:** no enrollment, completion or holder figure has been published [DE061]. Two people self-identify as holders; both work at Charities Aid Foundation-family sponsors, and an "inaugural cohort of 70" is unverified [DE062]. No job posting names DAFCP [DE063], and FINRA does not list it [DE064].
   - **Reception:** trade coverage was thin, and the only outside voices were mildly sceptical [DE069, DE070].

7. **Strategic reading.** The designation is not yet strategically important as a credential. It is informative as a **signal**:
   - the largest national sponsors are investing in adviser and sponsor-staff education through a neutral-branded academic channel;
   - complex-asset and noncash specialists are becoming the professional core of the field;
   - operational technology is outside the professional canon.

   Obtaining DAFCP is worthwhile for us only as a **low-cost research instrument** (one authorised enrollment to read the paid modules), not as a credibility signal (§8).

## 4. Strongly supported inferences

- **The programme's centre of gravity is sponsor-side practice, especially noncash and complex gifts.** Supporting evidence:
  - the faculty composition [DE033];
  - the curriculum's emphasis on complex assets [DE044];
  - the early holders being sponsor staff [DE062].
- **Sponsor exposure runs through three channels:**
  - lineage (the 2025 certificate);
  - money (the aligned 2027 Forum);
  - people (faculty).

  This overturns the earlier repository reading that it ran mainly through event money [DE072].
- **The curriculum's omissions match items on which the national sponsors compete or face criticism:** fees, payout distribution, portability and inactivity. With n = 1 curriculum this is an association, not proof of influence. Batch 37C found the same omissions in 11 adviser-education outlines, sponsor-tied and not ([queue-batch37-controls-education.md](../mission-2026-11-14/queue-batch37-controls-education.md) §4). So the gap is a field-wide pattern, not unique to DAFCP.

## 5. Working hypotheses (not established)

- **H1:** sponsor co-development carried from the 2025 certificate into DAFCP content. *Test:* interview the programme director and provost; obtain a content-governance policy (OQ01, OQ02).
- **H2:** the "inaugural cohort of 70" was a pilot cohort of sponsor and community-foundation staff before the public 2026-09-01 opening. *Test:* College confirmation (OQ09).
- **H3:** Module 6 surveys donor-facing portals and noncash-gift services rather than operational technology. *Test:* authorised enrollment review (OQ04).

## 8. Is it strategically important, and should we obtain it?

- **Importance:** Low-Medium today, rising only if the leading indicators in [10](10-strategic-scenarios-and-recommendations.md) appear. It matters most as a **map of who is shaping professional norms**: the four national sponsors and noncash administrators. It also shows where the canon is silent (technology, comparative economics).
- **Obtaining it:** recommended for **one team member as a research instrument** ($1,495, about 30 hours [DE018]). Clarity posture: Experimental (low impact, easily reversible). The purposes are:
  - read Module 6 and the legal-currency treatment (OQ04, OQ05);
  - see attribution of authorship (OQ02);
  - understand what holders will believe.

  Do not obtain it for credibility. Its market signal is currently weak [DE063, DE064, DE070].

## 9. Uncertainty that could change the conclusions (ranked)

## Syllabus corrections (2026-11-20) — governing

Source: [dafcp-syllabus-findings.md](../education/dafcp-syllabus-findings.md); evidence DE074–DE083. The syllabi were read under the [owner decision on robots.txt](../education/owner-decision-robots-2026-11-20.md).

1. **Required text.** The DAFCP 200 syllabus makes Ted Hart's *The DAF Revolution* (self-published 2025; ISBN 979-8-9997607-7-7) the required text. Hart is a DAFCP contributor. The book's listing carries endorsements from Vanguard Charitable's president and Ren's CEO. Ren therefore moves from "none found" to **informal-professional** (RM20).
2. **Contributors develop and teach the course.** The syllabus says course materials include lectures "delivered by the subject matter experts who contributed to the development of the course". The sponsor and administrator share among those experts (10 of 22) therefore bears directly on content.
3. **Charitable Solutions publishes a required CAP text.** CAP 549 requires a noncash-assets book published by Charitable Solutions LLC (DE080). That is a course-level commercial tie between the College and the programme director's firm.
4. **Topics covered.** The syllabus learning objectives cover sponsor fiduciary standards, impact investing and administrative best practices (DE076). The absence findings in §1 item 4 shrink accordingly. Payout, fees, portability, inactivity and AI remain unnamed.
5. **Exam.** 100 multiple-choice questions in 150 minutes, closed book, proctored, one attempt, no retakes (DE078).
6. **AI.** The syllabus addresses AI only as student conduct, and it bars sharing course content with AI platforms (DE079).
