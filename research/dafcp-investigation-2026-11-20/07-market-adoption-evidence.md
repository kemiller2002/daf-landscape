# DAFCP: market adoption and reception evidence

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Data:** [`adoption-evidence.csv`](../../data/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/adoption-evidence.csv). **Evidence:** [DE061–DE064, DE069–DE071].

## 1. Four measures kept separate

| Measure | What we found | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Enrolment** | No figure from the College on the programme page, in the launch release or in the Forum release. The checkout page showed "Sold out" on 2026-11-20 while the programme page still offered enrolment [DE025]. Its meaning is unknown, and it is **not** demand evidence | unknown |
| **Completion / designation holders** | Two people publicly self-identify, both in LinkedIn search snippets [R]. One works at Charities Aid Foundation's American Donor Fund; the other is CAF America's CEO. A third snippet names one more earner. An "inaugural cohort of 70 practitioners" is cited in these snippets and attributed to the College's CEO, but appears in no College publication [DE062] | unverified |
| **Employer requirement** | No job posting naming DAFCP was found. Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs and Glassdoor could not be opened [DE063] | none found |
| **Tuition reimbursement or endorsement** | No firm or sponsor has announced reimbursement, a requirement, or staff completion ([DE063]; relationship row RM26) | none found |

Additional indicators:
- **FINRA Professional Designations database:** CAP is listed; DAFCP is not [DE064].
- **CFP Board CE value:** not stated on the programme page and not confirmed (OQ06).

**Timing anomaly.** One self-claim appears to date from early September 2026. That is before the earliest completion a course opened on 2026-09-01 with an 8-week minimum would allow. Either there was a pilot cohort or the snippet date is wrong (working hypothesis H2; OQ09).

**Confusion with the predecessor.** Holders of the 2025 "DAF Certified Professional" badge, such as Omaha Community Foundation staff and a DAF-technology startup co-founder, are not DAFCP designees [DE021]. They should be excluded from any DAFCP count.

## 2. Who adopts first

Every visible early adopter, for both the 2025 badge and the DAFCP, works on the **sponsor or community-foundation side**:
- Charities Aid Foundation and CAF America;
- Omaha Community Foundation;
- College staff.

None is a financial adviser ([DE062]; [agent D table](../../data/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/adoption-evidence.csv)). This is the opposite of the launch framing, which addresses advisers first [DE016]. With n = 2 to 3, this is a signal, not a distribution [I].

## 3. Reception

| Source type | Items | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| Promotional (College, wire syndication, College staff on LinkedIn, ThinkAdvisor co-marketing webcast) | 7 | positive |
| Trade press repeating the release (InvestmentNews 2026-07-28; Financial Planning / American Banker 2026-08-06; an aggregator rewrite) | 3 | neutral to mildly sceptical |
| Independent assessment | 0 | — |
| Indifference signals: ThinkAdvisor's 261-designation roundup omits DAFCP; no Reddit threads; no comment from DAF critics (IPS, Madoff, ACCF) or from CFP Board, CGP, AFP, CoF | several | — |

The only outside comments, both in the Financial Planning piece, are mildly sceptical:
- Wealth.com/Stanford Law: DAFs are a small share of giving, and the material may belong inside broader charitable-planning education.
- Glenmede: "DAFs are the 'how'".

They question whether the credential is needed. They do not question its quality [DE069].

**Does it fill a genuine knowledge gap?** Public evidence cannot settle this. The gap claim comes from the College itself [DE016, DE049]. Substitutes exist and are free:
- sponsor CE;
- Daylight's "DAFopedia", an adviser-led knowledge hub [DE071].

Practitioner views would need interviews (OQ03, OQ07).

## 4. Limits

- LinkedIn content was seen only through snippets.
- Job boards were not opened.
- Archive.org page bodies could not be loaded, so earlier versions of the programme page could not be compared. Only capture dates are known [DE024].
- The course opened about 11 weeks before this observation, so low visible adoption is expected at this stage and is weak evidence on its own [I].

## 5. What to monitor (quarterly)

1. FINRA database listing.
2. CFP Board CE search entry.
3. College publication of holder counts or a DAFCP directory in its designation-verification tool.
4. "DAFCP" in job postings at community foundations and national sponsors.
5. Sponsor announcements of staff completion or reimbursement.
6. 2027 Forum attendance and agenda.
