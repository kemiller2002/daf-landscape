## 3. Readings

1. **DAFCP sits between CE and CAP.** It is about 30 times the length of a sponsor CE webinar, but roughly a third of CAP's coursework, with no experience gate [I from DE018 and DE066]. Its natural market is people who want DAF-specific depth without the CAP commitment, including nonprofit and sponsor staff (the $1,195 price).
2. **The CAP precedent sets expectations.** After more than 20 years, CAP is listed as "preferred" and never required, at community foundations and university gift-planning offices. A DAFCP *requirement* in hiring within its first years would be unprecedented for a College philanthropy credential [DE063] [I].
3. **The overlap with CAP is commercial as well as topical.** The Forum release advertises "pathways to" both DAFCP and CAP [DE039]. DAFCP may work as a lower-cost entry point into CAP [I; working hypothesis].
4. **Exam transparency is the cheapest credibility upgrade available to the College.** Publishing an exam blueprint, item counts and pass rates, as CFP Board does, would separate DAFCP from "alphabet soup" designations. This is a control point in [09](09-edf-assessment.md).
5. **Naming risk.** The 2025 badge called completers "DAF Certified Professional", which is nearly the DAFCP's own name. Public bios and at least one secondary summary already conflate the two [DE021]. Anyone checking adoption must check which credential a person holds.
