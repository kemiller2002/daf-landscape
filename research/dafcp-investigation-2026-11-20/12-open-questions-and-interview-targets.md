# DAFCP: open questions and interview targets

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Data:** [`open-questions.csv`](../../data/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/open-questions.csv) (OQ01–OQ11).

Questions are ranked by how much the answer could change a strategic conclusion. Priority 1 means the answer could reverse a conclusion in [01](01-executive-investigation-report.md) or [10](10-strategic-scenarios-and-recommendations.md).

**Rules for any outreach** (outreach was not performed in this investigation):
- Disclose who we are and why we are asking.
- Do not share unpublished repository findings without the owner's approval.
- Do not ask for course or exam materials.
- Treat answers as self-reports and corroborate them where possible.

## 3. Questions public sources cannot answer

- Paid curriculum content, the exam blueprint and pass rates.
- Enrolment and holder data.
- Sponsorship amounts and any content-review rights attached to sponsorship.
- How the College manages conflicts for named experts who work at sponsors or administrators.
- Practitioners' private view of the credential's value.
