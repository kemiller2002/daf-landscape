## 4. What the relationships do and do not show

**Documented:**
- The four largest national sponsors co-developed the predecessor programme and fund the companion event, which is explicitly organised around the DAFCP curriculum [DE020, DE037, DE038].
- People employed by a noncash DAF administrator, NPT and DAFgiving360 are among the named experts [DE026, DE031, DE032].
- The programme director holds a concurrent role at that administrator [DE026, DE027].

**Not documented:**
- Any sponsor funding of DAFCP itself.
- Any sponsor review or approval of DAFCP content.
- Any referral, licensing or marketing agreement.
- Any employer requirement or reimbursement.

**Alternative explanations for the ties [I]:**
1. **Expertise sourcing.** DAF expertise is concentrated in sponsors and their administrators, so any credible DAF course would draw faculty from them.
2. **Channel strategy.** Sponsors benefit when advisers and staff learn DAFs in a sponsor-friendly frame.
3. **Professional networks.** CAP alumni, Charitable Solutions and the College's CPSI have overlapped since at least 2001 [DE030].

All three are consistent with the evidence. Explanation 1 is the null hypothesis. Explanation 2 is supported for the Forum and the 2025 certificate, but not shown for DAFCP content.

**Disclosure gap.** A programme that markets itself as "neutral" [DE048] does not show its experts' employers, and no conflict-of-interest or content-governance policy is published for it. That is an observable governance gap. It is not evidence that content is biased.

## 5. Concentration observations

- **No national-sponsor faculty from Fidelity Charitable or Vanguard Charitable**, the two largest by assets [DE034]. Their documented role is institutional: the 2025 co-development and the 2027 Forum.
- **No DAF technology vendor has an institutional role.** Ren, TIFIN Give, Chariot, Daffy, Foundation Source and DonorFirstX/iPhi were all checked [DE040]. The repository's technology-led entrants are absent from this professional channel, while they do appear at an independent DAF summit [DE060].
- **The noncash cluster is commercially material.** Dechomai's payments to Charitable Solutions rose from $3.00m to $4.43m between TY2022 and TY2024 [DE029]. A curriculum that emphasises complex-asset gifts serves an audience that may later refer such gifts. **This is an incentive observation, not evidence of steering.**
