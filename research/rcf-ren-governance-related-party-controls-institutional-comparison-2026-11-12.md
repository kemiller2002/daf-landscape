# Batch 3B: RCF governance, Ren related-party controls, and institutional economics comparison
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Scope:** RCF governance and related-party approval; named legal-entity payees; Ren independent-sponsor payments; portability contract gaps.
**Evidence grade:** primary RCF Form 990 and audited statements; Morgan Stanley official disclosure. **Not** contract-level review.

## Executive findings
1. **RCF's 2024 Form 990 reports five voting directors, three independent voting directors, and zero employees.** Its Schedule O says management and administrative services, records, tax filings, staffing and professional resources are outsourced to Renaissance Administration LLC (Ren).
2. **RCF discloses a written conflict-of-interest process**: transaction-level interests recorded in board minutes; interested directors cannot vote; board must find transaction fair and reasonable; majority of nonconflicted directors must approve. This is documented governance design, **not** independent verification of how every transaction was evaluated.
3. **RCF's 2024 Form 990 Schedule L Part IV reports a $19,225,546 related-party administration transaction with Renaissance Administration LLC**, noting an officer overlaps. Schedule O lists it as the highest-paid independent contractor. This matches the $19,225,546 audited payment amount.
4. **Important legal-entity reconciliation:** audited Note 9 names Renaissance Acquisition Company LLC (Ren) as service provider; Form 990 identifies Renaissance Administration LLC, EIN 35-2111971, as contractor, preparer and Schedule L interested person. The same dollar figure strongly suggests a shared Ren service arrangement but does not establish legal succession, assignment or who ultimately received cash. Do not collapse distinct LLC names without a corporate records check.
5. **Morgan Stanley GIFT's official site identifies RenPSG as an unaffiliated back-office administrator**, while Morgan Stanley Smith Barney provides investment management. This confirms a second Ren customer model, but no verified named Ren payment amount or contractual fee percentage was obtained in this research batch.
6. **Exit/portability terms remain unknown**: the public audit and 990 do not reveal agreement duration, termination for cause/convenience, transition assistance, audit log export, data ownership, step-in rights, disaster recovery or change-of-control clauses.

## Governance mechanics and unresolved proof
| Control | Public evidence | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Independent oversight | 3/5 independent voting directors reported in 990 | Independence under every possible legal standard or effective challenge of pricing |
| Conflicted transaction vote | Schedule O: disclosure, recusal, fairness finding, nonconflicted majority | Minutes, alternative bids, comparable market pricing or compliance in any specific vote |
| Outsourcing | Ren manages administration, records, filings, staff | Whether sponsor retains sufficient independent technical and compliance expertise |
| Fee payment | 2024 Schedule L and audit both show $19,225,546 | Ren operating profit, cash flow to named ultimate corporate entity, Edward Jones portion |
| Sponsor approval | RCF says final grant authority remains with sponsor | Who physically clicks approval, which delegated personnel act, separation of duties and override logs |
| Exit rights | No contract text publicly verified | Whether exit is difficult, prohibited, expensive or well supported |

## Conflict-of-interest assessment
**Observed:** Related-party administrative service agreement; officer/director overlaps; almost all individual-fundholder administrative fee revenue paid to Ren under the 98% contractual formula; foundation reports no employees; governance recusal policy.
**Potential mechanism:** Sponsor depends on a related contractor for the operational data and staff used to assess contractor performance and fees. This may make independent verification and vendor replacement more complex.
**Counterevidence:** Three independent voting directors and explicit recusal/fairness rules. Outsourcing can be efficient and appropriate for a sponsor. No documented misconduct or breach.
**Unverified:** Competitive procurement, fairness opinion, benchmarking, board minutes, recusal in the specific Ren contract renewal, independent compliance resources and audit access.

## Financial normalization
RCF 2024 Form 990 Part III lists $19,617,095 program-service revenue and $19,225,546 Ren administration fees. Their ratio is ~98.0%, consistent with the audit's 98% of **specified administrative fee revenue** formula, but do not assert the Part III amount and contractual denominator are identical without detailed accounting notes.
**Do not** label the difference (~$391,549) 'RCF profit': program-service revenue classification, other revenues, other expenses and legal obligations differ.
Morgan Stanley GIFT: no verified Ren payee dollar amount. Do not use generic 'other fees for services' as a Ren proxy. Its independent sponsor model may have entirely different commercial terms.

## Clarity questions for next stage
1. Which Ren legal entity signed each agreement and received each payment? Resolve Renaissance Administration LLC / Renaissance Acquisition Company LLC / RenPSG.
2. What evidence supports 98% fee share being fair and reasonable? Request contemporaneous benchmarks and minutes.
3. Who provides independent sponsor compliance oversight and signs off on high-risk grants?
4. What are contract termination, transition service, data ownership, export format, retained audit logs and recovery clauses?
5. Are staff/board overlaps present at Morgan Stanley GIFT or only RCF? Do not generalize.
6. Can other institutional sponsor 990s/audits identify Ren as a named contractor with amounts? Distinguish contractor payments from broad expense classifications.

## EDF risk controls and empirical test
Design a **vendor independence test**: independent sponsor officers must be able to retrieve a random sample of grant evidence, reproduce approval rationale, reconcile grants payable and payment statuses, verify source-of-truth and initiate a mock provider transition without vendor-only credentials. Score source completeness, recovery time, data portability, approval traceability, and legal authority separation.
This is a proposed assessment, **not executed**.

## Decision
RCF is **not an undisclosed or uncontrolled related-party relationship**; its audit and tax return disclose the payments and governance framework. However, disclosure is not proof of arm's-length economics or effective independence. Ren's actual institutional customer revenue concentration remains unquantified.
