# Phase 1: Four-provider DAF comparison
Date: 2026-11-12. Preliminary, source-backed findings. Different reporting periods and entity scopes; do not treat as market shares.

## Vanguard Charitable
Calendar 2025: $3.96B granted, 267,838 grants, 66,785 unique charities, average grant $14,808. FY ended June 30, 2025 audited statements: $4,014,052 paid to Vanguard for investment recordkeeping and administrative services. This is not total platform cost.
Sources:
- https://www.vanguardcharitable.org/2025annualreport
- https://www.vanguardcharitable.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/vanguard-charitable-2025-financial-statements.pdf
Questions: identify all service providers, related-party agreements, grant approval workflow, per-grant cost, automation and advisor retention.

## Fidelity Charitable
Calendar 2025: $18.3B donor-recommended grants, approximately 3M grants, 395,515 donors, 226,823 charities. Report says 77% of grants went to nonprofits previously supported by the same account. Recurrence may create automation opportunities; actual review processes not yet established.
Source: https://www.fidelitycharitable.org/insights/2026-giving-report.html
Questions: repeat-grant approval, screening, operating expenses, grant processing channels, advisor relationship continuity.

## TIFIN Give
Company describes white-label multi-custody platform, advisor-managed options, AI features and wealth-enterprise distribution. TIFIN reported >$90M in grants and >15K grant transactions in 2024. Chariot describes roles: TIFIN Give technology, GiveClear Foundation sponsor, wealth firm program provider, Chariot disbursement. These are provider claims and partnership descriptions, not independently measured efficiency.
Sources:
- https://tifin.com/product/give/
- https://www.givechariot.com/resources/insights/getting-to-know-tifin-give-a-chariot-disbursements-partner-
- https://www.giveclear.org/reports-and-financials
Questions: confirm legal and financial relationships; unit economics, custody and data portability, AI feature outcomes, actual grant workflow, reliability and exceptions.

## National Philanthropic Trust
FY July 1 2024–June 30 2025: $6.44B granted, 159,543 grants, 20,561 accounts, median balance ~$79.7K and mean ~$2.70M. Large mean/median gap warns against interpreting average account economics as typical.
Source: https://www.nptrust.org/annual-reports/npt-annual-report/
Questions: institutional sponsor relationships, grant review, complex asset operations, account segmentation, unit economics.

## Preliminary synthesis
These are not interchangeable direct competitors: sponsors, wealth-oriented technology and outsourced disbursement occupy distinct layers. Avoid equating grant volume with platform efficiency or sponsor grant totals with technology provider throughput. Reporting periods differ. No architecture, operational latency, or cost-per-grant conclusion is yet supported.
