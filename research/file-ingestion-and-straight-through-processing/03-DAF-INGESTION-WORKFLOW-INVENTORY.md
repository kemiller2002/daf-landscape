# 03 — DAF ingestion workflow inventory

Research package: [file ingestion and straight-through processing](README.md) (2026-11-26). Data: [`workflow-inventory.csv`](../../data/file-ingestion-2026-11/workflow-inventory.csv).

Every distinct way information enters a DAF sponsor, across twelve domains, with the supplier, delivery, format,
artifact class, conventional effort (assumed minutes, used by the [economic model](09-ECONOMIC-ANALYSIS.md)),
automation and AI potential, the residual human role, and the redesign disposition. *Practice* is **confirmed** only
where at least one confirmed evidence row documents it; otherwise the row is **inferred** from adjacent practice and
DAF regulation.

**Artifact classes** (the controls differ by class): *command-batch* (requests to act: grant recommendations, allocation
changes, migration waves); *observation-set* (facts reported by an authority: bank statements, custodian positions,
returns); *reference-dataset* (whole public snapshots: IRS, OFAC, state registries); *evidence-document* (supporting
documents for a human decision); *configuration* (sponsor settings).

**Saturation.** The inventory stopped growing when further sources produced no materially distinct workflow: the last
passes (custodians, community-foundation software, corporate giving platforms) added evidence to existing rows and one
new row (state charity registries).

**Volume drivers are assumptions** set to reproduce the repository's existing activity benchmarks where they exist
(about 8 grants and 2 contributions per account per year, research/ai-native-daf-enterprise/09-financial-models); they
are not measured ingestion volumes (gap G1).
