# NPT, Stellar Technology Solutions and Ren: primary-source technology linkage
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Finding:** Historical NPT–Stellar web-platform relationship is supported by NPT-branded material hosted on a Stellar-controlled domain. Current contractual and architecture scope remain unverified.

## Direct evidence
1. NPT-branded **Donor Application**, form code 'NPT FOR DAFI 0920', served at https://npt.stellartechsol.com/npt/LinkClick.aspx?fileticket=bVwgpEam4jk%3D&mid=2534&tabid=656 . The document describes NPT's donor onboarding, including the online application at nptdaf.org. **The September 2020 form version is evidence of document date, not necessarily first hosting date.**
2. NPT-branded **Manage Your Donor-Advised Fund Account Online**, code 'NPT BRO MYDAFO 0420', served at https://npt.stellartechsol.com/npt/Portals/5/docs/Manage%20Your%20DAF%20Account%20Online%20%28NPT%29.pdf . The guide describes an interactive donor portal for secondary advisers and legacy plans. The April 2020 version predates the reported February 2021 GivingPoint launch. Domain hosting directly ties NPT-branded donor-facing materials to Stellar infrastructure; it does not alone establish authoritative ledger, vendor billing or source-code ownership.
3. Ren announced its acquisition of **Stellar Technology Solutions** on January 20, 2023, acquiring the **iPhi** enterprise software platform. Ren described iPhi as supplying general-ledger and philanthropic subaccounting capabilities. Sources: https://www.reninc.com/blog/ren-bolsters-philanthropic-tech-services-offerings-with-acquisition-of-stellar-technology-solutions/ ; https://www.reninc.com/blog/why-ren-and-stellar-are-combining/ ; https://www.capstonepartners.com/transactions/capstone-partners-advised-stellar-technology-solutions-on-its-sale-to-ren-inc/
4. NPT describes GivingPoint as its donor-facing portal; NPT's published launch interview describes a two-year development and implementation effort. Neither publicly attributes development to Stellar or Ren. https://www.nptrust.org/philanthropic-resources/philanthropist/five-things-to-know-about-npt-givingpoint-a-conversation-with-chris-adams-svp-of-it-solutions-and-cybersecurity/

## Clarity: what is established vs inferred
**Established:** NPT-branded 2020 donor application and interactive account guide reside at a Stellar Technology Solutions subdomain; Ren acquired Stellar in 2023.
**Strong inference:** NPT had some historical portal or technology-service relationship with Stellar. Direct hosting supports an operational relationship, but precise contract terms and technical ownership remain unknown.
**Not established:** Stellar developed GivingPoint, Ren developed GivingPoint, iPhi is the current authoritative NPT ledger, NPT currently pays Ren, or Ren has current operational responsibility for all NPT DAFs.
**Counterhypotheses:** (A) Stellar supplied an earlier NPT donor portal replaced by GivingPoint; (B) Stellar backend persisted behind NPT-built GivingPoint; (C) Stellar supplied hosted documents or discrete modules only; (D) a program-specific arrangement existed rather than a sponsor-wide platform.
**Revision trigger:** contemporaneous NPT/Stellar contract, authorized architectural documentation, release notes, named vendor testimony, or verified production-system attribution.

## EDF diagnostic boundaries
NPT legal sponsor → donor interface (pre-2021 Stellar-hosted materials vs GivingPoint) → underlying fund/accounting system → custodian positions → grant approval → payment/recipient settlement. Determine whether GivingPoint is a replacement interface, system of record, or integration layer; identify changeover/migration controls and historical reconciliation. No incident or control failure is inferred.
