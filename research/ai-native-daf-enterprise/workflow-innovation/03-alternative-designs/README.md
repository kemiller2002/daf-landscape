## Strongest and radical candidates (summary)

- **Adopt now (AI-native):**
  - D-W6-3 (structured transfer with full history);
  - D-W7-3 (delegation tokens; L3 in the parent PoC);
  - D-W8-3 (replay migration);
  - D-W10-3 (evidence by construction);
  - D-W11-3 and D-W11-4 (continuous reconciliation plus unique keys);
  - D-W12-3 and D-W12-4 (progressive onboarding; account crystallizes at intent).
- **Radical, forwarded:** D-W7-4 (invisible embedded DAF: distribution thesis), D-W9-4 (plan-at-opening: needs a field pilot), D-INV-4 (index-only, no affiliated menu), D-INST-4 (program-as-code).
- **Radical, held:**
  - D-W6-4 (atomic re-titling): legal only with sponsor-segregated custody; viable intra-platform.
  - D-W8-4 (sponsor-owned record substrate): a Model C product thesis.
  - D-W10-4 (continuous external assurance): needs auditors.
  - D-ORG-4 (decision-rights market).
  - D-GOV-4 (governance by exception).
- **Rejected:** D-ACQ-2 (lead scoring on third-party wealth data: covert profiling).
