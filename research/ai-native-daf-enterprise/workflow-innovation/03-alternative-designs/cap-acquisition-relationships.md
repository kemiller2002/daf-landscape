# Capability: donor acquisition and relationships

**Outcome (BO-51, BO-56):** donors who would benefit from a DAF find one at low cost and receive honest guidance, including when a direct gift is better. From 2026 a DAF loses for non-itemizers (direction-donor-tax-scenarios-2026; strategic synthesis §0 A).

**Evidence:**
- The fastest-growing sponsors are channel- or platform-led [I] (strategic synthesis §0 E).
- CAC is unpublished (09 model [A]).

| ID | Type | Mechanism | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-ACQ-1 | Conventional | Brand marketing, tax-season campaigns and advisor wholesaling; a funnel from awareness to application | Observed |
| D-ACQ-2 | Automation-first | Marketing automation, lead scoring and content personalization | L1 |
| D-ACQ-3 | AI-native | Contextual assistance at moments of need (a liquidity event, year-end planning) through advisor tools; an honest suitability check that may recommend *no DAF* (deterministic suitability policy: functional area A) | L1 |
| D-ACQ-4 | **Radical: no marketing function. Acquisition is a by-product of partner workflows** | The DAF is offered only where a partner's own process surfaces a charitable intent: the advisor's planning tool, an estate attorney's software, an employer equity plan, a crypto exchange's donate button. There is no funnel and no brand campaign; the "sales" step is a partner API call | L1 |
| D-ACQ-5 | Ecosystem: referral network with community foundations and nonprofits | Nonprofits and community foundations refer donors who want DAF features; revenue shared under fee-transparent terms | L0 |

**Constraint pass:**
- **No covert profiling** (privacy; repository ACT-002 eliminated).
- **No commission-based solicitation** under the charitable broker-dealer exemption (REG-030), which constrains D-ACQ-5 referral fees. This needs counsel.
- **Tax claims must be accurate** (consumer protection).

**Perspectives:**
- **Economist:** D-ACQ-4 lowers CAC but cedes margin to partners.
- **Commercial:** D-ACQ-4 equals Model C/D distribution.
- **Customer advocate:** the suitability check builds trust.
- **Adversarial:** partner dependency.

**Recommendation:** D-ACQ-3 plus D-ACQ-4. E11 tests CAC sensitivity.
