# Vendor testing access requests — Ren and TIFIN Give

**Prepared November 11, 2026. Not sent.** These requests support public, buyer-side research and should be sent only by an authorized party who will honor vendor terms, confidentiality, data restrictions and security limits.

## Ren test access request

Subject: Request for controlled evaluation of DAF donor tax-planning capabilities

Hello,

We are conducting a comparative evaluation of donor-advised fund platforms, with an emphasis on identifying appropriate charitable planning opportunities, modeling alternative contribution methods, and providing a complete audit trail.

We have reviewed Ren's public DAF Benefit Estimator and advisor materials. Would Ren be able to provide an authorized sandbox or structured demonstration of the current tax estimator and any advisor-facing opportunity identification or tax-lot selection capabilities?

We would use exclusively synthetic data and would like to evaluate:

- Inputs and outputs of the Benefit Estimator, including 2026 charitable deduction rules, filing status, AGI, itemization, direct-donation alternatives, carryovers and fees.
- Whether an advisor can identify opportunities across permitted client portfolios, rather than relying on individual preselected gifts.
- In-kind transfers, donor consent, sponsor acceptance and donation substantiation.
- Current product/module applicability, version, audit evidence and data-access controls.

We can supply a concise synthetic test matrix. We would not ask for confidential customer information, proprietary algorithms or credentials outside an approved test environment.

Please advise whether this scope can be supported, what authorization and terms are required, and which tax capabilities are outside the product's intended scope.

Thank you.

## TIFIN Give test access request

Subject: Request for controlled Tax Intelligence evaluation

Hello,

We are conducting a comparative evaluation of DAF donor opportunity identification and tax-planning workflows, with particular interest in TIFIN Give's announced Tax Intelligence capabilities.

Would TIFIN provide an authorized demo or test environment using synthetic donor holdings, including multiple tax lots, restricted securities, loss positions and incomplete financial-planning data?

We seek to observe:

- The actual opportunity identification workflow, lot selection and portfolio deviation/restriction controls.
- The model's treatment of 2026 deduction rules and direct-gift alternatives, including cases where a DAF is not appropriate.
- Human approval, donor intent, data provenance and permission boundaries.
- Single-Charity Fund legal eligibility checks before any qualified charitable distribution claim.
- Model/product version, API or export options, and available audited or practitioner-reviewed validation.

We can provide a limited synthetic case set for the demonstration. We are not seeking private client records, privileged operational materials, nonpublic algorithms or security bypasses.

Please confirm access options, terms, and which functions are presently available to advisors.

Thank you.
