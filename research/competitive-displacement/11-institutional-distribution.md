# 11 - Institutional distribution

Status: research (2026-11-26). Sources: [trackF-segments.csv](data/evidence/trackF-segments.csv), [trackF-notes.md](data/evidence/trackF-notes.md) (SRC-067); prior segment analysis (SRC-007); switch log (SRC-065). Question: is distribution, rather than technology, the primary barrier to entry?

## Answer

**Yes, on the evidence available (HYP-001, Medium confidence).** Four independent lines point the same way:

1. **Incumbent revenue is entangled with distributors' revenue.** RCF passes ~98% of fundholder administration fees to Ren (CLM-017); TIFIN pays advisers 0-100 bps; Morgan Stanley earns $4.24M as adviser to GIFT; Commonwealth reportedly receives up to 5 bps (search snippet only). A technically better entrant cannot match this without matching the economics.
2. **Distributors add rather than replace.** SEI kept its Ren-run fund and added TIFIN; Confluence works with several sponsors; Commonwealth stayed on Ren through the LPL conversion (CLM-018).
3. **Channels are being locked up now.** TIFIN Give + SEI (banks and trust companies, Jan 2026), TIAA + Charityvest + Foundation Source (Sept 2026), Altruist + Endaoment (Oct 2026), Orion and Envestnet + GiftingNetwork (2025). Small DAF technology firms that tried banks and credit unions were acquired (Amicus by TIFIN, Pinkaloo by Ren) (CLM-025).
4. **Where technology does drive switching (community foundations), the buyer wants a broader product** (integrated fund accounting) and chooses an incumbent suite (Foundant) (CLM-016).

The counter-evidence: no win/loss data exists (CLM-026; OQ-001), so the inference rests on behaviour, not stated reasons.

**No segment has a published CAC** (CLM-026). PR #88 and PR #86 used $125k per sponsor (ASSUMPTION); that figure is untested.

## 3. Implications

- **Technology is necessary, not sufficient.** Entry requires either an incentive-compatible distribution arrangement or an entry point where distribution economics do not dominate.
- **Distribution partners** that could carry an AI-native platform without competing directly with Ren: CF-software or administrator partners at re-platforming windows; distributors to banks and trust companies similar to SEI (FIS/Fiserv wealth units; partner status unknown); multi-custodian data layers without a DAF partner (BridgeFT already taken by Foundation Source; Addepar open); credit union league foundations and CUSOs (back-office need plausible, member demand unproven).
- **Expect long cycles and assurance gates**: banks and credit unions demand SOC 2 Type 2 history that an entrant lacks for 12-24 months (CLM-040).
