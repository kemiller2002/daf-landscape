# Next expansion wave: provider evidence and executable diligence plan
**Prepared November 11, 2026 | All product tests presently unexecuted**

## Why expand

The commercial buyer chooses an **operating model**, not merely an app. Compare:
- **Sponsor + full administration outsourced** (Ren, Foundation Source, GivingTech; also NPT private label).
- **Tech-only outsourcing with sponsor retained** (Ren, Foundation Source, GivingTech claimed).
- **Advisor-led white-label with sponsored charity** (TIFIN/GiveClear/Chariot; Foundation Source/Charityvest; NPT).
- **Donor-owned relationship to sponsor (not donor-owned donated assets)** (Fidelity, DAFgiving360, Vanguard, Daffy, Charityvest).
- **Corporate donation processing and payroll matching** (Benevity/AOGF, Blackbaud Giving Fund, Givinga CSA).
- **Infrastructure** (Chariot payments; BridgeFT custodian data; PG Calc planned giving).

Require a comparison **within buyer type and operating model**. A retail sponsor's low donor fee does not establish a lower technology vendor cost, or the existence of a licensable standalone core platform.

## P2: GivingTech DAFtech

1. Obtain **legal name/EIN of every U.S. charity** offered under “Your Brand, Our Charity” and distinguish any non-U.S. models. A website brand called DAFtech is not proof of tax-exempt sponsor entity [U17].
2. Require one independent **U.S. institutional operating reference**, exact product SKU, full funds flow, anonymized ledger, security assurance, vendor contract and observed actual time/cost.
3. Reproduce full synthetic contribution → legal sponsor acceptance → unitization → grant vetting → approval → ACH/check → recipient settlement → bank reconciliation.
4. Independently test cost reduction percentages before using “up to 60%” as a result; obtain actual comparison denominator and total cost.
5. Test AI assistance, grant approval segregation, PII retention, staff override, and API/idempotency logic.

## P4: Infrastructure and corporate giving

- **Chariot:** separate DAFpay checkout from Disbursements, obtain net pricing for each, measure actual received grants and exception handling [U23,U39,U40].
- **BridgeFT:** test data-latency stamps, stale lots, custodian feed outages, RBAC and reconciling entries; published data pipelines are not necessarily intraday [U34,U35].
- **Benevity/AOGF/Blackbaud Giving Fund/Givinga:** investigate the legally distinct workplace / corporate giving model and cash conversion, rather than ranking raw grant volume against full investment-advised DAFs [U16,U20,U36,U37].
- **PG Calc:** review planned giving capability and historic SOC claims as a separate system, not proof of Foundation Source DAF assurance [U32,U33].

## Common due diligence test matrix

| ID | Test | Pass evidence |
|---|---|---|
| EV01 | Map 501(c)(3) legal sponsor and any technology service provider | EIN, audited charitable financials, signed service division of labor |
| EV02 | Reconcile DAF assets and vendor revenue | Charity assets separate from vendor GAAP revenue, double counting eliminated |
| EV03 | Verify SOC reports | Current in-scope independent audit report, criteria, period, opinion, exceptions, subservice orgs |
| EV04 | Evaluate sponsor-owned fund ledger | NAV, subledger, errors, custodian files, trial balance, post-close corrections |
| EV05 | Grant lifecycle and separation of duties | Sponsor approves, disbursement provider executes, reversal and receipt confirmed |
| EV06 | Multi-custodian data freshness | Observed source timestamps, missing-lot handling, stale price warnings |
| EV07 | Privacy and donor/adviser roles | Tenant isolation, consent, cross-client access negative test |
| EV08 | Tax lot and 2026 donor suitability | Correct direct-vs-DAF comparator, floor, QCD exclusion, professional escalation |
| EV09 | Historical portability | Five years of reports/receipts exported, reconciled and accessible post-cutover |
| EV10 | Switching and revenue quality | Contract term, retention cohorts, implementation services, NRR and invoiced unit cost |
| EV11 | Marketing scale validation | Separate sponsor accounts vs company supported accounts vs paying enterprise customer logos |
| EV12 | Actual per-grant economics | Billable grant, payment success, exceptions, staff minutes, fraud and bank pass-through |

Every vendor currently has **not_tested** results in this newly expanded protocol. Public product demonstrations and press releases are **evidence of claims**, not execution tests.

## Commercial sourcing and confidentiality

Only publish independently accessible information and explicitly authorized demonstrations. Do not include non-public GLG/Guidepoint/AlphaSights client identities, prior employer trade secrets, customer contracts subject to NDA, investment firm due diligence, or personally identifiable donor information. Published agreements can constrain competitive benchmarking; obtain permissions before using credentialed environments.
