# Migration and integration tooling: competitive and economic findings (2026-11-26)

**Status:** public-source research, no interviews. Prices are vendor list prices as accessed on 2026-11-26. Where a price could not be confirmed on a vendor page it is marked *unverified*. Findings are hypotheses for OPP-10 ("Data migration and continuity", `data/commercial-opportunity/ai-native-capital-minimization/opportunities.csv`). They are not demand validation.

**Origin.** This is a by-product of the Transitus research initiative. Transitus is a proposed embeddable F# data-integration engine for Echelon Foundry; the full architecture is in `kemiller2002/transitus`. The Transitus competitive research (20 products, 128 sources) is the primary source here: `kemiller2002/transitus:docs/research/01-competitive-research.md`. This folder keeps only the findings that matter to DAF market strategy.
