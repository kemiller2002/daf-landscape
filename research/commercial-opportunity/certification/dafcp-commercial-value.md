## 2. Credential-use constraints (trademark)

The College's *Guidelines for Designation Trademark Use* (PDF dated 2025-08-04, read 2026-11-20 from `theamericancollege.edu/knowledge-hub/guidelines-for-trademark-use`) [F] list CAP, ChFC, RICP and other marks. **They do not yet list DAFCP**, because they predate it. The rules, which can be expected to extend to DAFCP [I], are:

- "These designation marks may only be used by an advisor who has earned the credential". Use is allowed only after the Registrar notifies the holder.
- The acronym may appear only directly after the holder's name, with the ® or ™ symbol.
- "No designation mark … may be used as part of a business name, or as part of a web URL."
- The marks "may not be used to mislead consumers".
- Use must stop immediately on disciplinary action or non-compliance with the recertification programme.
- A footnote crediting the College's ownership is required near the first use, except on stationery and business cards.

**Implications for Echelon Foundry [I]:**
- **The credential belongs to an individual, never to the firm.** "Echelon Foundry, a DAFCP firm", a DAFCP-branded product, or a URL containing the mark would breach the guidelines.
- Marketing could truthfully say "[Name], DAFCP™, leads our DAF practice" once the person holds it.
- The firm cannot claim that its research or software is "DAFCP-certified".

## 3. Commercial-value analysis

| Possible benefit | Evidence for | Evidence against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Credibility with sponsors** | It is the only DAF-specific designation (DE068). No sponsor requires the credential | Weak positive. Shared vocabulary and a "we learned your field's canon" signal, but not a differentiator for technology or operations work |
| **Credibility with wealth managers and advisers** | Launch framing targets advisers (DE016) | CFP/CAP dominate. Advisers buy from people with wealth-platform and integration experience. No adviser-side adoption evidence (DE062) | Weak |
| **Credibility with investors** | — | Investors pay for evidence and access to data, not designations. No evidence that due-diligence buyers screen for credentials [I] | None expected |
| **Higher fees** | — | No evidence in this repository that certified consultants charge more. A request for pricing benchmarks is in the competition research | Unsupported |
| **Access to otherwise unavailable opportunities** | The College's DAF Forum (March 2027) gathers sponsor staff, gift planners and advisers. DAFCP holders get "pathways" messaging (DE039). Speaking or writing for the College could follow | Forum attendance is open to non-holders. Premier sponsorship is a paid relationship that would compromise independence (DE037) | Moderate *network* value through the Forum and faculty network. That value does not depend on the credential |
| **Referral network** | 22 named experts across sponsors, administrators, law firms and consultants (DE033). Holders share a directory, implied by the recertification programme's YourAdvisorGuide listing [I] | The early network is small (2 visible holders). The relevant experts can be reached without the credential | Small, optional |
| **Research access** | Enrolment gives access to the paid modules, the authorship list and the textbook. This answers open questions OQ02, OQ04 and OQ05 | — | **Real, and the main value** |
| **Reputational risk** | — | The programme's sponsor-side lineage and framing (DE020, DE046). Holding it could be read as aligning with incumbent sponsor views [I] | Small if disclosed and framed as research |

## 4. Bottom line

- **DAFCP mainly signals general familiarity with the subject.** There is no evidence that it produces measurable commercial value in 2026: no employer recognition, no fee premium, no buyer screening on it.
- **Recommended:** one Echelon Foundry principal enrols. The purposes are research access and shared vocabulary with sponsor staff. The cost is $1,495 plus about 30 hours, and $240 a year if kept. Clarity posture: **Experimental** (low cost, easily reversible).
- **Not recommended:**
  - building marketing positioning around the credential;
  - paying for DAF Forum sponsorship;
  - creating a competing certification. There is no evidence of unmet demand (DE068, DE070).
- **Possible education partnership.** If interviews show the College lacks operations or technology content (OQ04), a guest lecture, case study or white paper on DAF operations and AI controls is a lower-risk way to gain visibility than sponsorship. It must be governed so that Echelon's independence is stated.

## 5. Signals that would change this assessment

| Signal | Effect |
|---|---|
| Any sponsor, broker-dealer or RFP lists DAFCP as "required" or "preferred" | Upgrade the credential to a market-access asset |
| CFP Board CE approval or a FINRA listing | Moderate upgrade for adviser-facing credibility |
| College-published holder counts above several hundred | A meaningful network effect; revisit |
