# Contradiction register

**Rule:** no contradiction here is silently resolved. "Recommended" means this program's recommendation. Every status is **Open – owner decision** unless it says otherwise. Severity uses a 1–5 scale: 5 = could cause irreversible financial loss, legal exposure or unauthorized action; 3 = could cause a material misstatement or a build defect; 1 = editorial.

Decision authorities: **Sponsor board** (delegation of grant authority); **GC** (counsel); **CFO/Controller**; **CRCO**; **Product owner** (repository owner, for requirement-text questions); **Architecture owner**.
