## Answer in brief

1. **Cash to first referenceable invoice from a completed Unbound (S3)** is in the key figures (revision 3, governing configuration: M1 deep review performed by Echelon, demand-driven) and in the bases table for every configuration and for revision 2. Revision 3 is lower than revision 2 and still well above agent AB's published figure, because funded obligations (TPSP, control operations net of maintenance, a realistic first conversion) remain.
2. **No single-sponsor sequence reaches self-sufficiency in base under either basis.** The partner-book sequence pays back in base only when the sponsor performs the deep review (P3-DEC-MOD005) or under M5 (OD-CDD-02) (P3-CLM-MOD007).
3. **The gate threshold depends on the review allocation**: break-even in single-sponsor accounts and in partner-book accounts is in section 1b. With Echelon performing M1 reviews it is not reached; with the sponsor performing them it falls to about 3,000 sponsor accounts or a book of about 12,000 accounts (P3-CLM-MOD017).
4. **The registry and review-allocation decisions (P3-DEC-MOD005 with P3-DEC-MOD004) are the largest levers under the owner's control.**
5. **Outside investment is not justified before the gate** (P3-CLM-MOD012).
