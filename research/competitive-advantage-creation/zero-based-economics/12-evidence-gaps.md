## Legal uncertainties kept open
These are costed as counsel time, not resolved:
- **Third-party sender / TPSP role under Nacha.** The sponsor is the Originator and Unbound never holds money (CNV-LEG-001, CNV-PAY-001). Obligations as a TPSP still need counsel.
- **Source rights for shared charity intelligence** (UNB-CNET-011). Non-redistributable sources must stay out of shared stores.
- **Whether CSL, UK Sanctions, SAM and FAC screening is legally required** (UNB-CDD-CIV-004). Candidate sources stay advisory until counsel adds them.
- **IP assignment mechanics under OD-P3-01** ("EF owns all property we make"). The mechanics need counsel.

- Evidence tests V-1..V-9 have not run, and no requirement here bypasses them.

- Kill-rule and commercialisation clocks start when DAF commercialisation becomes its own cost centre (OD-P3-03/04). This package spends nothing and so starts no clock.
