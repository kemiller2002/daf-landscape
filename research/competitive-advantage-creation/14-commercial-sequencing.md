## Answer in brief

Two bases are reported side by side (section 5b): **revision 2 (conservative)**, as published after round 1, and **revision 3 (CFO-corrected)**, which drives the other tables. Revision 3 limits contingency to its stated basis, makes deep reviews demand-driven and per sponsor, nets control operations against maintenance, caps on plausible sponsors' live customers, and signs the first customer whole (P3-CLM-MOD018).

1. **The decision does not depend on the basis.** Under both bases and every registry configuration, the kill rule pauses every platform sequence at month 24 when the DAF cost center opens at month 0, with about $1.3-1.8M spent; on the kill-rule Monte Carlo no sequence beats doing nothing. Gate DAF-specific spend on a named partner (P3-DEC-MOD003).
2. **Whether any route can pay back depends on who performs the human deep review.** If Echelon performs M1 reviews (vendor cost), nothing pays back at any size. If the sponsor performs them, as licensing and the r1 sponsor policy imply (new owner decision P3-DEC-MOD005), or OD-CDD-02 allows M5, the **partner-book sequence** pays back in base; single-sponsor A3 still does not, because only a handful of plausible sponsors are large enough (P3-CLM-MOD007, P3-CLM-MOD017).
3. **The kill-rule result is timing-sensitive.** With a whole first signing, a go-live lag of 8 months or less, or the cost center opened at month 6 or later (which the gate implies), A3 passes the pilot test (P3-CLM-MOD006).
4. **Few plausible single sponsors exist.** Excluding donation processors and national sponsors presumed to run their own platforms leaves 17 FY2024 sponsors with at least 2,646 accounts and 10 with at least 5,291 (P3-CLM-MOD015). A partner's book is the larger addressable unit.
5. **Building outside the cost center is not a saving** (A3X versus A3B; P3-CLM-MOD016), and **outside investment is not justified before the gate** (P3-CLM-MOD012).

## 7. Kill rule (OD-P3-02, OD-P3-03, OD-P3-04)

**Rule as modelled.** No named partner by cost-center month + 6 (inclusive), or no referenceable live pilot by cost-center month + 24, pauses the thesis. The pilot is the first month in which one whole Unbound sponsor customer is live; its buyer security review is passed at contract because first regulated signing cannot precede SOC 2 Type 2 issue (P3-ASM-MOD036). At a pause, a funded wind-down is charged if any customer has signed (P3-ASM-MOD038).

**Cost-center month (P3-ASM-MOD017, P3-DEC-MOD001).** The first month in which a sequence needs DAF-specific cash spend booked to its own budget. Founder time does not open it (it is counted in fully loaded cost). Development is excluded in the base case and included as a sensitivity. F never starts the clock. **Transitus (OD-P3-02)** is kill-exempt and does not open the DAF cost center.
