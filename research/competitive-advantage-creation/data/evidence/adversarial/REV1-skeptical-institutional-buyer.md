# REV1 - Skeptical institutional buyer

Status: adversarial review, round 1 (2026-11-26). Reviewer panel REV1. Perspective: CFO of a university foundation (or a large hospital foundation) asked by the advancement VP to consider launching a DAF programme on Unbound. Written independently of the other REV1 perspectives. Challenge IDs P3-CHL-REV1011..REV1018 in [adversarial-challenges.csv](../../fragments/REV1/adversarial-challenges.csv).

## Bottom line

The package is unusually honest that my segment has no demonstrated demand, and then builds its preferred non-displacing route (P3-DEC-CD001) on me anyway. I would not be a design partner on this evidence. My board would ask four questions the package cannot answer: will donors use it, what does it cost us in total, who carries the legal exposure, and what happens to our donors' money if the vendor stops at month 24.

## 1. The market count is organizations, not buyers (P3-CHL-REV1011, high, judgment)

The "qualified core" of 600-800 education and health foundations ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):152) becomes "High in count" expansion potential for Sequence E ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):67). The same report says no public evidence shows these institutions want a programme ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):155), and P3-CLM-CD004 records that the existing 165-174 education and health sponsors hold about 4,000 accounts in total (about 24 each) and that their market share fell from 1.38% to 1.11%. The barrier matrix rates our perceived demand "H" because national DAFs already deliver grants to us free ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):59). An addressable count of 600-800 with a demand rate of unknown and a segment in relative decline should not appear as "high" potential anywhere. Proposed change: replace "High in count" with "count known, demand unmeasured; recent adoption rate in the segment" and compute the segment's observed launches per year from the FY2021-24 panel entries.

## 2. My programme would be tiny, and your model already says that cannot carry you (P3-CHL-REV1012, high, judgment)

At about 24-50 accounts my licence would be the $13k minimum ([16](../../../16-capital-efficient-market-entry.md) licensing table). Your model says sequences whose customers pay $12.5-60k never pay back (G: peak $6.13M, none in 120; E: $4.46M; [14](../../../14-commercial-sequencing.md):45-48), yet P3-DEC-CD001 still calls new-programme enablement "the evidence-preferred non-displacing acquisition route" ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):115). From my chair, being the reference customer of a route the vendor's own model calls uneconomic means I should expect a price rise, a forced migration to a host, or a vendor exit. Proposed change: P3-DEC-CD001 should say explicitly that the route does not fund the vendor and state the minimum fee or programme count at which it would.

## 3. The programme break-even omits most of my real cost (P3-CHL-REV1013, medium, judgment)

The minimum-viable-programme grid gives an existing charity break-even at 15-46 accounts at $133k balances ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):21; F = $13-40k, [05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):94). Excluded or thin:
- one-time onboarding is excluded from F (§3.1);
- programme legal documents, gift-acceptance policy changes and 4966/4967 review procedures (counsel, UNKNOWN);
- incremental external audit scope for a new restricted-fund class;
- investment custody and unitization of DAF assets inside our endowment pool, and the custodian feed ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):64);
- integration with our advancement CRM and gift-processing system;
- staff time for the sponsor-reserved approvals the package says cannot be removed (~25 min per account-year in E10, [10](../../../10-structural-cost-elimination.md):183).
None is measured (PAR-018/019 time study absent, [05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):219). Proposed change: add a "first-year programme cost to the institution" line with these items as ranges before presenting break-even counts to any buyer.

## 4. The regulatory exposure lands on me, and it is unfunded (P3-CHL-REV1014, medium, judgment)

If we run our own programme we are the sponsoring organization: 4966/4967 responsibility, pending regulations (88 FR 77922 still proposed, [05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):208), donor-benefit questions that are acute for us (gala tickets, naming, scholarships for donors' relatives), and fund-definition questions for scholarship-type funds. The package correctly says counsel must confirm, and then puts no cost on it for the buyer side. "Hosted programme roles... map onto that split; this is INFERENCE and needs counsel review" ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):212). For a buyer this is the first purchase objection, not a footnote.

## 5. Continuity: what happens to my donors at month 24 (P3-CHL-REV1015, high, judgment + factual)

The vendor's kill rule pauses the thesis if there is no referenceable live pilot by month 24, and in the Monte Carlo 75% of A3 draws are paused ([19](../../../19-sensitivity-and-stress-analysis.md):93); G (the direct-foundation sequence) is paused at month 24 in the base case ([14](../../../14-commercial-sequencing.md):48). The model writes off revenue after a pause ([14](../../../14-commercial-sequencing.md):276), which tells me nobody has planned what happens to live customers. Continuity of a sponsor's tenancy on partner exit is "missing in Unbound requirements" ([12](../../../12-institutional-distribution.md):88, P3-CLM-FGJ020). Unbound is a requirements baseline with no ledger code ([07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):25). I would need source escrow, a funded wind-down obligation and a tested full-history export before signing; the package costs escrow at about $0 ([02](../../../02-existing-unbound-economics.md):130). Proposed change: add a customer-continuity obligation (escrow, wind-down period, export rehearsal) as a cost line in every sequence and as a condition of P3-DEC-CD001.

## 6. Your timing to my first invoice is understated and internally inconsistent (P3-CHL-REV1016, medium, factual)

Report 06 says new-programme strategies reach first revenue in "about 6-18 months" from S4 with incremental cash "in the tens to low hundreds of thousands of dollars" ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):13). The model puts cash from S4 to first invoice at $953k base ($221k-$4.21M) with 21 months base ([16](../../../16-capital-efficient-market-entry.md):54). S4 does not exist; from today (S2) it is 30 months and $1.75M ([16](../../../16-capital-efficient-market-entry.md):52). Report 06's figure should be corrected or explicitly reconciled; a buyer reading 06 alone would expect a launch in under 18 months.

## 7. Proven alternatives already meet my need (P3-CHL-REV1017, medium, judgment)

NPT private label (14-16 weeks), DAFtech (8 weeks), Givinga ($12.5k base, 30-45 days), hosting on our community foundation (Ohio Wesleyan with The Columbus Foundation) ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):43; [07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):56-59). These carry SOC 2 Type II reports and live references. The package's own advantage register finds nothing that is not ordinary, temporary or unproven ([07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):9-19). "Replayable eligibility decisions" is an auditor feature I have never been asked for. The package should state, for the S1 buyer specifically, which measured benefit would make me prefer Unbound over a private-label sponsor; at present there is none.

## 8. The BNY Mellon CGF "trigger" is unverified (P3-CHL-REV1018, low, factual)

The host-exit buying trigger rests on BNY Mellon CGF having voted to end its programme in December 2024, with the affected programmes' status "not checked" (P3-GAP-CD005; [05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):177). It is used as a trigger in [06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):43 and 190. One unverified event should not be cited as a purchasing trigger until the status of the hosted programmes is checked.
