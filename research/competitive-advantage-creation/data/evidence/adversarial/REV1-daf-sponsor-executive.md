# REV1 - DAF sponsor executive

Status: adversarial review, round 1 (2026-11-26). Reviewer panel REV1. Perspective: CEO of a mid-sized community foundation (a few hundred DAFs, an incumbent fund-accounting suite, a board finance committee and an annual audit), also asked to consider becoming a "host sponsor" for other institutions' programmes. Written independently of the other REV1 perspectives. Challenge IDs P3-CHL-REV1031..REV1037 in [adversarial-challenges.csv](../../fragments/REV1/adversarial-challenges.csv).

## Bottom line

The package asks me to do two things: license a ledger that does not exist yet, and run a second system so that universities and hospitals can put their donors' funds under my legal responsibility for a few thousand dollars a year each. The savings it shows are against a staffing baseline I do not have, and under a licensing model some of the work it removes from the vendor lands back on my staff.

## 1. At my scale the savings are small and the risk is large (P3-CHL-REV1031, high, judgment with repository data)

The event model shows a system cost of $118 per account-year against a "conventional" $267 ([18](../../../18-economic-model-methodology.md):87). The median community foundation in the repository's own FY2024 panel has 47 DAFs (SRC-013, `data/mission-2026-11-14/daf-long-tail-sponsors-fy2021-2024.csv`, my computation; median of all sponsors 30.5). Even granting the full $149 gap, 47 accounts save about $7k a year, against conversion staff time (E24 one-time 9,000 minutes plus 2 per account even after automation, [10](../../../10-structural-cost-elimination.md):70), board approval and audit re-familiarization. Against a well-automated incumbent the package itself puts the gap at $11-71 per account-year ([07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):94), i.e. $0.5-3.3k a year for me. The research should show sponsor-side payback by sponsor size band using the actual account distribution, not a 10,000-account sponsor (the size used for the $677k vetting saving, [18](../../../18-economic-model-methodology.md):73), which outside the nationals barely exists.

## 2. Licensing moves work back to my staff (P3-CHL-REV1032, medium, judgment; partly conceded)

Report 10 says technology-only licensing moves all residual minutes (~53) to the sponsor at x1.4 efficiency (~74 sponsor minutes) and that system cost rises to $197 against $167 for AI-native administration ([10](../../../10-structural-cost-elimination.md):87). The licence route DEC-006 prefers is therefore the one that raises my labour relative to outsourcing. The package reports this correctly in report 10 but the sponsor-facing value case (reports 06 and 16) never states it. Proposed change: every sponsor-facing value statement should quote the sponsor's own residual minutes and cost under licensing, not the system cost.

## 3. The host-sponsor factory recommends a route the model rejects (P3-CHL-REV1033, high, factual inconsistency / circular)

P3-DEC-CD001 proposes "new-programme enablement through a host sponsor (S1 and S4, Sequence E variant)" as the evidence-preferred route ([06](../../../06-customer-acquisition-without-displacement.md):115). The model gives Sequence E a base peak of $4.46M with no payback in 120 months, 100% of Monte Carlo draws without payback when uninterrupted, and a pause at month 24 under the kill rule ([14](../../../14-commercial-sequencing.md):45; [19](../../../19-sensitivity-and-stress-analysis.md):79). Report 16 then says small-programme licences "do not carry a licensor on present assumptions" ([16](../../../16-capital-efficient-market-entry.md):246). Both recommendations sit in the same package without reconciliation. "Evidence-preferred" here means "preferred among non-displacing routes because it does not displace", which is circular when the economic test fails. From my side, the factory also assumes I will: run a second system for hosted funds (P3-HYP-CD002 untested), accept 4966/4967 responsibility for other institutions' donors, and do so for programme fees of $2-25k a year ([05](../../../05-untapped-institutional-markets.md):122). Proposed change: P3-DEC-CD001 must cite the E/G results and either be withdrawn as a revenue route or reframed as a credibility channel with an explicit loss budget.

## 4. Change-driven re-vetting savings depend on my auditor and my policy (P3-CHL-REV1034, medium, judgment)

Report 11's hybrid M5 at ~$124k against ~$767k for a human 180-day review ([11](../../../11-continuous-charity-intelligence.md):12) compares against a full human review of 20,000 charities every 180 days that I do not perform. The automated renewal requires my delegated policy (SPD-GRT-001) and auditor acceptance (V-9, untested); audit preparation stays at 7,200 minutes a year until then ([10](../../../10-structural-cost-elimination.md):70). The sample audit (149 per stratum, ~$7.6k) is new detective work that someone must perform and evidence. The accountable owner (0.15-0.5 FTE, [10](../../../10-structural-cost-elimination.md):186) is presumably the vendor's for the shared layer, but the eligibility decision remains mine. Proposed change: label the M1 comparison as against a hypothetical conventional review, and show the comparison against what a typical sponsor actually does today (release-time checks plus incumbent vetting), which the package has not measured.

## 5. Vendor survival is the first thing my board will ask about (P3-CHL-REV1035, high, factual from model outputs)

With the kill rule applied, 75% of A3 Monte Carlo draws pause and 76% show no payback within 120 months; the median economic outcome is -$765k ([19](../../../19-sensitivity-and-stress-analysis.md):93). Report 14's statement that A3 "passes under every cost-center definition tested" ([14](../../../14-commercial-sequencing.md):264) is about the base path only, and report 16's answer-in-brief omits the paused share. For a sponsor, a 75% chance that the vendor's own rule stops the business within two years (conditional on the assumption ranges) is decisive. That is a disclosure issue, not a modelling error: the figure exists but is not presented where decisions are summarized.

## 6. The price assumption in the winning sequence is the one report 13 says fails (P3-CHL-REV1036, medium, factual inconsistency)

A3 uses $81 per account gross less 30% channel share (P3-ASM-AB030; ER-7 basis). Report 13 says viability under a moderate response requires the headline "≥ ~$100-120" through a partner, and that at the $81 reference a 40% partner share fails ([13](../../../13-incumbent-response-model.md):85, 88). The V-2 pass rule is ≥$100 per account ([04](../../../04-low-cost-commercial-validation.md):10). So A3 succeeds at a price below the validation gate the package itself set. Either A3 should be run at the V-2 price with a correspondingly lower win rate, or V-2's threshold should be explained as not required for A3.

## 7. Migration evidence does not exist yet (P3-CHL-REV1037, medium, judgment)

My switching decision depends on a full-history export and money tie-out. Transitus is at phase 0 with no production code ([09](../../../09-independent-component-commercialization.md):35; [07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):35), V-4 needs my incumbent's cooperation, and the package concedes the incumbent controls the export (CHL-005). "Reproducible migration" (EK010) is listed among the strongest candidates ([07](../../../07-architectural-competitive-advantages.md):104). Until one real rehearsal reconciles to the cent, it should not appear on any sponsor-facing list of strengths.

## 8. The first-invoice headline in report 02 is superseded but not annotated (P3-CHL-REV1038, low, factual)

Report 02's answer-in-brief still states $0.25M / $1.0M / $3.5M cash to first invoice from S3 ([02](../../../02-existing-unbound-economics.md):22). The model's corrected figures, after adding the registry run AB omitted, are $256k / $1.05M / $4.39M, and cash to self-sufficiency is $1.24M base rather than $1.47M ([16](../../../16-capital-efficient-market-entry.md):19, 22). The adverse tail is 25% worse than 02 says. Report 02 should carry a forward pointer to the corrected figures so that the earlier number is not quoted alone.
