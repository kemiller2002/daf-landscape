# 24 - Cross-repository implications

Status: research (2026-11-26). Reconciliation agent (RECON). This report lists every Phase III cross-repository implication (P3-XRI-) in [cross-repository-implications.csv](data/registers/cross-repository-implications.csv). All are **proposed (not approved)**. None has been submitted to, or changed, the target repository from this package. Each target repository's own governance decides whether to accept it: Praxis for transitus and echelon-foundry; the convergence baseline for unbound; README conventions for echelon-organization-administration. Research is cited by repository-relative path and is never copied into product repositories (BRIEF). These proposals are distinct from Phase II's XRI-001..018, which the owner authorized to progress subject to their conditions ([01](01-approved-strategic-baseline.md)).

## Answer in brief

1. **Thirteen proposals:** eight from the research agents, four added after model revision 2 (P3-XRI-RECON001..004) and one after round 2 (P3-XRI-RECON005). Targets:
   - unbound: nine;
   - transitus: two;
   - echelon-organization-administration: two;
   - none for the general-purpose methodology repositories, in line with BRIEF's rule against putting DAF logic in them.
2. **The two most consequential proposals are P3-XRI-RECON005 and P3-XRI-RECON001.**
   - **P3-XRI-RECON005 (round 2).** It asks Unbound to state that who performs the human deep review is sponsor policy under licensing (RR-REQ-X-007-r1; UNB-CDD-CIV-010). An Echelon-performed review would be a separately priced service, never an implicit platform cost. This allocation decides break-even: 3,079 accounts if the sponsor performs the review, not reached if Echelon performs M1 (`gate-break-even.md`).
   - **P3-XRI-RECON001.** It records the cost consequence of OD-CDD-02. Revision 2 put the registry at about $768k against about $124k a year at 20k charities, and break-even at 10,963 against 3,949 accounts. In revision 3, an Echelon-performed, demand-driven M1 review adds about $469k a year for one sponsor.
3. **Two other new proposals put the model's dependencies where their owners can act:**
   - P3-XRI-RECON002: Transitus conversion hours and package reuse set the implementation rate after customer three.
   - P3-XRI-RECON003: the owner's cost-center definition and funding capacity set the cash at risk, $1.33M for A and $1.38M for A3 before the kill rule pauses (revision 3; $1.46M and $1.81M in revision 2; `sequences-kill-rule.md`).
4. **Proposals that need no new capability come first:**
   - P3-XRI-HI005: start archiving IRS releases now;
   - P3-XRI-RECON001, RECON003 and RECON005: record owner decisions;
   - P3-XRI-FGJ001: sponsor continuity on partner exit.

## 3. Sequencing and conditions

- **Can proceed on owner acceptance, at no cost:**
  - P3-XRI-HI005: the archive, because IRS files overwrite history;
  - P3-XRI-RECON001, RECON003 and RECON005: annotations that record decisions (RECON005 also states the sponsor-policy allocation of the deep review);
  - P3-XRI-RECON004: the TPSP cost statement and the 2027 group-exemption surge.
- **Conditional:**
  - P3-XRI-HI004 needs P3-VAL-HI005;
  - P3-XRI-HI002 and HI003 depend on OD-CDD-02 and V-9, because tiers and sample audit matter only if automated renewal is allowed;
  - P3-XRI-FGJ002 waits on P3-DEC-FGJ001 and P3-DEC-FGJ003.
- **Measurement proposals** (P3-XRI-HI001, P3-XRI-RECON002) pay off only once there is live operation or a real rehearsal. Their value is that evidence then accumulates from day one.

Nothing here authorizes implementation, expenditure or production change (BRIEF status ladder). A proposal accepted by its repository reaches at most "Requirements incorporated" there.
