# Batch 1C: Verified affiliated-provider economics in Fidelity and Vanguard DAFs
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Research register:** M01, M02, R04. **Status:** Primary audited evidence; full fee waterfall still incomplete.

## Executive finding
Two major national DAF sponsors explicitly document economic ties with affiliated financial-services providers. This is stronger evidence than inferring monetization from sponsor branding, but **reported payments and product allocations are not equivalent to affiliate profits**.

## Fidelity Charitable, FY ended June 30 2025
Source: https://www.fidelitycharitable.org/content/dam/fc-public/docs/fc/audited-financial-statements-2025-2024.pdf (audited consolidated financial statements, Statement of Activities p4 and Note 7 p24).
- $85,863,974,267 end net assets.
- $179,875,837 administrative fee expense, $84,317,489 investment-management fee expense.
- **Note 7:** Trustees contracted with FMR LLC, parent of Fidelity Investments, and affiliates for administrative and investment services. Under the contract the Gift Fund **paid FMR amounts equal to the administrative fee the Gift Fund charged donors, net of tier price credits**.
- Service providers named: National Charitable Services LLC (administration/recordkeeping); Fidelity Brokerage Services LLC (brokerage/custody); Strategic Advisers LLC (appointed SEC-registered investment adviser). Mutual funds may be managed/advised by FMR affiliates.
- **Caution:** Note 7 confirms the contractual fee formula, but the consolidated expense line cannot simply be declared FMR's total profit, nor can all $84.3m investment management fees be assigned to FMR without additional allocation evidence. Affiliate underlying fund expense ratios may be reflected in NAV and not the same line.
- Donor subaccounts and adviser-managed accounts are not distinct legal entities; donated assets legally belong to the Gift Fund.

## Vanguard Charitable, FY ended June 30 2025
Source: https://www.vanguardcharitable.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/vanguard-charitable-2025-financial-statements.pdf (audited financial statements, Note 1 p6).
- $27,212,476,535 end net assets; $3,503,325,979 grants; $8,592,521 management/general; $26,353,488 program; $4,091,664 fundraising.
- **Explicit related-party service fees to Vanguard:** $4,014,052 FY2025 versus $3,916,396 FY2024 for investment recordkeeping and administrative support, included in functional expenses (do not add them to total expenses again).
- **In-kind professional services contributed by Vanguard:** $1,000,342 FY2025 versus $788,129 FY2024; contributions, not paid fees.
- **35 of 37 investment pools** consist of Vanguard mutual funds or ETFs, per Note 1; two other pools from RhumbLine and Hamilton Lane. This demonstrates affiliated product distribution, not the dollar amount or margin of underlying fund fees.
- Vanguard provided a non-interest-bearing financing arrangement, amount and repayment terms require Note 6 extraction; no valuation of economic benefit attempted.
- Vanguard Charitable is a legally separate nonprofit with trustee discretion, not a customer-owned Vanguard brokerage account.

## The new business-model distinction
**Affiliated captive channel:** Sponsor directs most or all donor investment pools into related manager products and pays affiliates for administration/custody (Fidelity, Vanguard; different mechanics).
**Open adviser channel:** Sponsor approves external adviser, who may preserve some advisory fees on sponsor-owned assets (AEF, Fidelity CIAP, DAFgiving360, NPT).
These models can coexist. An adviser retaining a client relationship is not proof that the sponsor or adviser retains every dollar of investment fee.

## Clarity: conclusions and counterclaims
- **Supported:** Fidelity's audited Note 7 documents a fee formula paid to FMR affiliates. **Not supported:** all Fidelity Charitable fee expenses are Fidelity corporate profit.
- **Supported:** Vanguard's Note 1 documents $4.014m related-party support fees and 35/37 affiliated pools. **Not supported:** $4.014m is all economic benefit Vanguard Group receives from charitable assets.
- **Supported:** NPT has a large investment management expense category. **Not supported:** this is revenue to Ren, any named technology vendor, or NPT itself.
- **Supported:** donor assets become sponsor property. **Not supported:** donors control the sponsor's investment mandates.

## EDF mechanism
Donor funds charitable sponsor→sponsor pays related/unrelated service providers→assets allocated to related/unrelated investment funds→investment fund expenses and custodian charges→grants. A single corporate family can benefit from administration, custody and asset management, but accounting presentation differs and layers may overlap. The key control is **entity-specific beneficiary tracing**: amount, service, legal entity, period, accounting treatment, source, and residual uncertainty.
