## 1. Summary

1. **The firms with revealed interest are a small, connected set.** Four groups have put capital or products into DAF organizations in 2023–2026:
   - private-equity owners of the two administration platforms: Bain Capital, Valeas and Great Hill for Ren; GTCR for Foundation Source;
   - wealth platforms that launched or rebuilt DAF programs: SEI, AssetMark, Envestnet, Orion, Altruist and LPL;
   - the affiliate-linked nationals (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard);
   - consumer-giving platforms that became sponsors (GoFundMe).

   Sources: [Bain Capital, 2023-07-26](https://www.baincapital.com/news/ren-secures-strategic-growth-investment-led-bain-capital-tech-opportunities); [Great Hill, 2025-10-28](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251028702541/en/Great-Hill-Partners-Announces-Strategic-Growth-Investment-in-Philanthropy-Tech-Platform-Ren); [GTCR/Foundation Source, 2023-09-13](https://foundationsource.com/newsroom/press-releases/gtcr-announces-acquisition-of-foundation-source/); [SEI, 2026-10-28](https://www.seic.com/about-sei/newsroom/sei-launches-sei-charitable-fund-bringing-charitable-giving-advisor-and-client-wealth-experience); [Altruist DAF launch, 2026-10-06](https://www.investmentnews.com/goria/custodian/altruist-adds-donor-advised-funds-with-no-minimums-for-advisors/268481); [GoFundMe Giving Funds, 2025-06-30](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250630362413/en/GoFundMe-Launches-Giving-Funds-to-Make-Charitable-Giving-Easier-for-Everyone).

2. **The biggest 2026 signal is Vanguard–Altruist.**
   - Vanguard signed a definitive agreement to acquire Altruist on 2026-08-26. Terms were not disclosed, and press estimates of about $4bn are unconfirmed ([PLANADVISER, 2026-08-26](https://www.planadviser.com/vanguard-buying-altruist-in-new-deal/); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/vanguard-to-acquire-altruist)).
   - Six weeks later, Altruist launched a DAF program sponsored by **Endaoment**, not by Vanguard Charitable. Fees start at 50 bps with no minimums ([InvestmentNews, 2026-10-06](https://www.investmentnews.com/goria/custodian/altruist-adds-donor-advised-funds-with-no-minimums-for-advisors/268481)).
   - [Clarity: inference, M] So a large parent that owns an affiliated sponsor is, for now, letting an acquired custodian partner with an independent sponsor-of-record. Whether that survives integration is an open question (P-BUY-01).

3. **Common PE ownership has not yet produced captive distribution.** Two cases:
   - **Bain Capital** owns a majority of Ren (2023) and owns Envestnet (closed 2024-11-25, about $4.5bn) ([Ren/Bain release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230725652545/en/Ren-Secures-Strategic-Growth-Investment-Led-by-Bain-Capital-Tech-Opportunities); [Envestnet, 2024-11-25](https://newsroom.envestnet.com/2024-11-25-BAIN-CAPITAL-COMPLETES-ACQUISITION-OF-ENVESTNET)). Yet in September 2025 Envestnet chose **Foundation Source**, Ren's rival, as its philanthropy integration ([Foundation Source, 2025-09-08](https://foundationsource.com/newsroom/press-releases/foundation-source-partners-with-envestnet-to-expand-advisor-access-to-philanthropic-technology-and-services/)).
   - **GTCR** owns Foundation Source (2023) and AssetMark (closed 2024-09, about $2.7bn) ([AssetMark](https://www.assetmark.com/?p=7867)). AssetMark's DAF partner is **TIFIN Give / GiveClear**, announced June 2024 ([InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/assetmark-and-tifin-give-forge-daf-partnership/254585)).
   - [Clarity: inference, M] Portfolio-company independence, or contracts signed before the deal, outweighs sponsor-level synergy so far. Both are natural places for a future cross-sell or combination (P-BUY-02).

4. **Wealth platforms mostly partner or white-label rather than acquire.** In 2024–2026 the observed pattern among strategics is contracts, not takeovers:
   - SEI runs two stacks: RCF/Ren and GiveClear/TIFIN ([SEI Giving Fund portal](https://sei.donorfirstx.com/); [SEI Charitable Fund](https://www.seic.com/about-sei/newsroom/sei-launches-sei-charitable-fund-bringing-charitable-giving-advisor-and-client-wealth-experience)).

   - Orion partnered with GiftingNetwork ([Business Wire, 2025-08-18](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250818952704/en/Orion-and-GiftingNetwork-Partner-to-Enhance-Charitable-Giving-Solutions-for-Advisors-and-Clients)).
   - SS&C ALPS white-labels TIFIN Give ([Business Wire, 2025-11-19](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251119360194/en/SSC-ALPS-Advisors-and-TIFIN-Unveil-Innovative-Donor-advised-Fund-Program/)).
   - Betterment and Evergreen Wealth integrate Daffy ([Business Wire, 2024-11-20](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20241120656554/en/Betterment-Expands-Charitable-Giving-with-Donor-Advised-Funds-from-Daffy); [Business Wire, 2025-11-18](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251118231735/en/Evergreen-Wealth-Strengthens-Tax-Optimization-Platform-with-Daffy-Partnership-Bringing-Donor-Advised-Funds-to-Clients)).
   - TIAA uses Charityvest with Foundation Source ([TIAA, 2026-09-28](https://www.tiaa.org/public/about-tiaa/news-press/press-releases/2026/09-28)).
   - The only strategic purchase of a DAF-adjacent rail in the record (Shift4/The Giving Block) was written down within a year ([Shift4 FY2022 10-K financial report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1794669/000179466923000006/Financial_Report.xlsx)).

5. **Disclosed multiples for DAF-specific assets are essentially nonexistent.** The Giving Block is the only deal with disclosed consideration: $54m upfront plus up to $246m of earnout, with a $(47.1)m fair-value cut in FY2022 ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/shift4-to-buy-2-companies-for-579m/619624/)). Every Ren, Foundation Source, Vennfi, Giving Place, Stellar, PG Calc and Pacific Foundation Services price is undisclosed ([`company-funding.csv`](../../data/mission-2026-11-14/company-funding.csv)). Adjacent platform prices are public: Envestnet about $4.5bn, AssetMark about $2.7bn, LPL–Commonwealth about $2.7bn, Benevity "more than $1bn". None of these is a DAF multiple.

6. **Who probably buys next, and at what stage** [Clarity: inference, M–L]:
   - (a) A **secondary PE sale or recapitalization** of Foundation Source. GTCR has held it since 2023-09 and has run a four-deal add-on program.
   - (b) **More PE layering at Ren**, after a 2023 majority deal and a 2025 growth round.
   - (c) **Wealth platforms buying white-label stacks**. TIFIN Give is the most separable asset: TIFIN has already sold one philanthropy unit (Giving Place) to refocus ([TIFIN, 2025-04-30](https://tifin.com/news/give-a-tifin-company-refines-focus-on-scaling-donor-advised-fund-solutions-with-giving-place-acquisition-by-foundation-source/18235/)).
   - (d) **Nonprofit-software consolidators** (Bonterra, Blackbaud, Bloomerang) buying DAF payment capability rather than integrating it. Chariot is the obvious target, but nothing indicates it is for sale.

   Sponsors themselves cannot be bought. They merge or affiliate (§3).

## 2. Method and scoring rubric

**Universe.** 89 rows (firms, or grouped firms where none showed interest) in five categories (§4–§8), seeded from the brief and the repo graph, then extended by web searches on 2026-11-14 for deals, investments, launches, partnerships and executive statements dated 2020–2026. Firms with no evidence found are kept in the CSV with score 1, so that absence is recorded rather than hidden.

**Evidence classes** (revealed > stated > inferred):
- **R (revealed):** capital or a binding commitment in a DAF organization or DAF product, such as an acquisition, investment, a sponsor or program launch, or a signed white-label or technology contract.
- **S (stated):** a public partnership announcement without capital, an executive statement, a product feature, or an integration listing.
- **I (inferred):** adjacency only, such as portfolio overlap or the capability to enter, with no DAF action found.

**Interest score, 1–5** = evidence points (R=3, S=2, I=1) + recency (+1 if the latest qualifying event is on or after 2024-11-14, i.e. within 24 months) + capacity (+1 if a deal of at least $1bn, or a fund or balance-sheet signal of that size, is documented for the firm or its parent; **counted only for R-class firms**, so size alone cannot lift a stated or inferred interest). Capped at 5. Components are shown in the CSV column `score_components`.
- *Limitation:* capacity favours large firms. For example, Benevity scores 5 on capacity plus recent partner activity, not on any sign that it wants to buy a DAF organization. Score measures *likelihood of further action*, not quality of fit.
- Fit is scored separately, H/M/L, for each of the five DAF segments.

**Segments** (repo convention):
- **PA:** platform and administration vendors (Ren, Foundation Source, TIFIN Give, Give Interactive, GiftingNetwork, Foundant).
- **RL:** payment and grant rails (Chariot, PayPal Grant Payments, Ren RDE, Stripe or ACH).
- **AS:** adviser-channel sponsors (RCF, GiveClear, NPT, AEF, Charityvest, Endaoment as sponsor-of-record).
- **CS:** consumer sponsors (Daffy, GoFundMe Giving Fund, Groundswell, the nationals' direct channel).
- **VD:** charity verification and data (Candid, Benevity validation, PayPal confirmed charities).

## 3. The legal boundary: you can buy the vendor, not the sponsor

- **What a sponsor is.** A DAF sponsor is a §501(c)(3) public charity. Donated assets are its property, and donors keep only advisory privileges ([IRS: donor-advised funds](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/donor-advised-funds); [26 U.S.C. §4966](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4966)). There is no equity to acquire.
- **Three routes for a commercial firm to gain control of a DAF franchise.** Each is evidenced:
  1. **Buy the vendor that runs the sponsor.**
     - Ren administers RCF, and RCF pays Ren 98% of individual-fundholder admin fees, $19.23m in FY2024 ([RCF FY2024 audited statements](https://renaissancecharitable.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2024RCFAuditedFinancialStatements.pdf)).
     - Foundation Source bought Vennfi, the technology behind Charityvest, while Charityvest Inc. stayed independent ([Foundation Source, 2024-10-17](https://foundationsource.com/newsroom/press-releases/foundation-source-acquires-vennfi-the-fintech-powering-donor-advised-fund-charityvest/)).
     - PE has used this route every time.
  2. **Create an affiliated sponsor under service agreements.**
     - DAFgiving360 is an independent nonprofit with service agreements with Schwab subsidiaries ([ThinkAdvisor, 2024-06-21](https://thinkadvisor.com/2024/06/21/after-25-years-schwab-charitable-has-a-new-name/?amp=1)).
     - GoFundMe Giving Fund is "separate and independent" from GoFundMe, Inc., which supplies its services ([GoFundMe newsroom](https://www.gofundme.com/c/newsroom/gofundme-launches-giving-funds)).
     - Daffy Charitable Fund is powered by Aside Inc. ([`company-funding.csv`](../../data/mission-2026-11-14/company-funding.csv)).
  3. **Contract with a sponsor-of-record** (white-label or private-label):
     - Altruist with Endaoment ([InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/goria/custodian/altruist-adds-donor-advised-funds-with-no-minimums-for-advisors/268481));
     - SEI, AssetMark, Zions and Alera with GiveClear and TIFIN ([TIFIN/Alera](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112726739/en/Alera-Group-Partners-with-Give-a-TIFIN-platform-to-Launch-White-Labeled-Donor-Advised-Fund-Platform-for-Clients));
     - Edward Jones, LPL and others with RCF ([Ren LPL page](https://www.reninc.com/lpl-rcf-daf/)).
- **Charity-to-charity combinations** need board action and, in many states, attorney-general review. Example: the New York AG approved the Orange & Sullivan / Rockland community foundation merger, effective 2026-01-01 ([Rockland Times, 2026-02-06](https://rocklandtimes.com/2026/02/06/community-foundation-of-orange-sullivan-and-rockland-launches)). NCF grows by bringing local foundations into its affiliate network, for example WiseHeart becoming NCF Memphis ([NCF Memphis](https://www.ncfgiving.com/memphis/the-story-of-ncf-memphis/)).
- **Constraints that follow** [Clarity: inference, M–H]:
  - (i) A vendor's revenue depends on sponsor contracts that sponsor boards can re-bid. The SEI dual stack and AEF's move to Ren show that channels and sponsors do switch ([AEF/Ren, 2025-07-23](https://www.aefonline.org/thought-leadership/american-endowment-foundation-selects-ren-as-a-strategic-partner-to-expand-philanthropy-drive-innovation-and-foster-growth-in-financial-services/)).
  - (ii) Excess-benefit and private-benefit rules limit how much value a commercial affiliate can extract from a sponsor. Fees must be arm's length; see the fund-manager track's regulatory-mismatch caveat ([fund-manager track](../fund-managers-daf-cross-business-opportunities-2026-11-11.md)).
  - (iii) Asset-based adviser fees on DAF assets (55 bps at OneGive, 50 bps base at Altruist) are the commercial glue, and they depend on sponsor permission ([OneAscent ADV](https://oneascent.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/25/2025/11/20260811-OAWM-ADV-Part-2A.pdf); [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/goria/custodian/altruist-adds-donor-advised-funds-with-no-minimums-for-advisors/268481)).

### 8.2 Pattern and implications

1. **Stage.** PE enters DAF technology at the **growth or buyout stage of an already-profitable administrator**, then runs add-ons that buy sub-scale tech at seed-to-Series-B maturity (Vennfi, Stellar, Giving Place, Amicus.io). No PE firm has funded an early-stage DAF startup in the record. [Clarity: inference, H; see also [failed-startups P2](failed-daf-startups-deep-research.md)]
2. **Strategics partner first.** Every wealth platform that added DAFs in 2024–2026 did so by contract (§5.1). The ones that later *bought* something bought the **distribution platform** (Vanguard to Altruist, LPL to Commonwealth), and the DAF program came along. [Clarity: inference, M–H]
3. **Hold-period arithmetic.** Incline held Foundation Source for about 3.7 years. GTCR is at about 3.2 years in Nov 2026. Bain has held Ren for 3.3 years, with a partial sell-down to Great Hill. [Clarity: inference, M] A Foundation Source sale or recap in 2027–2028 is consistent with that history. No process has been reported.
4. **Rails remain venture-held.** Chariot ($11m A), Daffy and Endaoment are VC- or grant-funded and unacquired. The one strategic rail purchase (TGB) was impaired. [Clarity: inference, M] Expect rails to be bought by nonprofit-software consolidators or payment firms only with earnouts, or to stay independent with many integrations, as Chariot has (Bloomerang, OneCause, GoFundMe, TIFIN).
5. **Sponsors consolidate by affiliation and merger, slowly.** Recent sponsor-level combinations in the record are local CF mergers and NCF affiliates, not national roll-ups.
