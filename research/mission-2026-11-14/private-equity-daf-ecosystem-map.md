With Deed, Bonterra competes directly with Benevity.

6. **Likely next transactions** [Inference] (§7):
   - **Open exit windows on long holds:** Orion (TA since 2015), Neon One (FTV since 2018) and Bloomerang (JMI since 2020).
   - **Benevity:** Hg's exit window opens 2026–2028.
   - **Bonterra:** Apax exits or recapitalizes 2027–2028, after more add-ons.
   - **Foundation Source:** GTCR sells or recapitalizes 2027–2029.
   - **Ren:** a later Bain sell-down.
   - Give Interactive and GiftingNetwork are add-on candidates, but their ownership makes them hard to buy. Chariot or Daffy late-stage rounds are possible, but no evidence was found.

## 2. Method

- **Company-first.** I built a universe of about 70 companies across nine layers (data dictionary §10) from the brief and the repo files. For each, I searched for owners, investors and lenders with dates, preferring sources in this order:
  1. company, PE-firm or lender releases;
  2. SEC filings and 990s;
  3. law-firm and adviser deal notices;
  4. trade press;
  5. aggregators, which are marked as such.
- **Evidence classes** (`evidence_class` column):
  - **P1:** issuer, PE firm, lender, SEC or 990 primary source.
  - **P2:** deal-counsel or financial-adviser announcement.
  - **S:** trade press or secondary reporting.
  - **A:** aggregator snippet, unverified.
- **Roles** (`role`):
  - `majority`, `minority`, `co-investor`, `lender`, `venture`;
  - `growth_undisclosed`: a growth investment where control is not stated;
  - `add_on`: acquired by a PE-backed platform;
  - `strategic_acquirer`;
  - `owner_unspecified`;
  - `none_found`.
- **Hold periods.** Exit windows assume a 4–6-year hold from entry, the typical range for buyout and growth funds. Each is labelled **[Inference]** and adjusted for observed signals such as add-on pace, secondary sales or new co-investors.
- **Limits.**
  - Paid databases (PitchBook, Debtwire, LevFin) were not used, so private-credit coverage is thin.
  - Several Form ADV Schedule A/B checks and BDC full-text searches were not completed; they are queued as P-PE-02.
