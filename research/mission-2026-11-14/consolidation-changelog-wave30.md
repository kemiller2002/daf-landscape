## 5. Main corrections carried into part (d)

- **International share:** 5.6% → 7.8% (N31, wave 30). Understated by 2.2–2.6 points, not 2.2–2.5.
- **Donate pages:** any DAF option 40.3% → 41.2% (75/182), labelled a lower bound (43.4% counting four universities); DAFpay 13.4% → 13.7% (waves 24B, 26).
- **NPT FY2023 $22.84bn:** a real filed value and most likely a filer error. With Part X net assets, FY2023 CR5 is 53.8% instead of 52.9% (wave 29).
- **Top-10 donor export:** NPT documents an Excel export (wave 23). This partly meets the existing F trigger for A8.
- **Give Interactive:** $41.80bn of known clients at 2025 asset levels; $32.59bn without certificate-log hosts (wave 23).
- **Fidelity callback:** published, discretionary (wave 22).
- The other rows are direction-level wording and scope fixes: CF fee multiple 3.6–4.7x, 803 CF filers, GoFundMe letter signatories, MS GIFT reserve range, Blackbaud Copilot, Givebutter boundary, pool-return floor, tax carryover reading, Ohio State scope and others.
