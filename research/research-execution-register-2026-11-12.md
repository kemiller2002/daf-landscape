# DAF Research Execution Register: Wealth Management, Economics, Technology, Experience, Regulation
**Created:** 2026-11-12
**Purpose:** Durable, prioritized execution register for the next research phase. This is an additive index to [comprehensive backlog](comprehensive-research-backlog-2026-11-11.md), not a replacement. Each ID is persistent; preserve IDs when updating status.

## Status conventions
- **BACKLOG:** Research not yet executed.
- **FOUNDATION:** Initial study exists, but evidence gaps remain.
- **IN PROGESS:** Active source collection (use IN_PROGRESS in future edits).
- **EVIDENCE_COMPLETE:** Defined source collection finished, not necessarily a supported conclusion.
- **CONCLUSION_READY:** Evidence reviewed, alternative explanations tested, conclusions and limitations recorded.
- **BLOCKED:** Missing required data, authorization or access.
Research cannot be marked complete just because a hypothesis or a plan was written. For every item record source links, dates, sample, contrary evidence, Clarity audit, EDF diagnosis, uncertainty, commercial implications and next falsification test.

### Evidence gate for every completed batch
1. At least one primary-source document for every material factual assertion, with as-of dates.
2. Distinguish published program eligibility from confirmed active usage, contracts and Pave eligibility.
3. Include contrary evidence and quantified uncertainty; don't invent unavailable data.
4. Separate recommendation, payment, settlement and receipt; sponsor, donor, adviser and vendor economics.
5. Identify buyer, beneficiary, payer, authority, commercial feasibility and strongest disconfirming fact.
6. Cross-reference existing research rather than rewriting completed reports.
