# NPT–Ren relationship verification: GivingPoint and underlying administration
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** Open investigation. User-provided lead that Ren developed NPT's platform is not independently verified by public evidence reviewed here.

## Finding
No public primary source located in this pass explicitly attributes development of NPT GivingPoint or its authoritative fund accounting platform to Ren, Renaissance, RenPSG, Crown Philanthropic Solutions, DonorFirstX, Pinkaloo or iPhi.

NPT's February 12, 2021 GivingPoint launch interview describes a two-year development and implementation project, with NPT CIO Christopher Adams responsible for technology, operations and system selection. It does not name the software developer or implementation vendor.

Ren's public discussion of DAF outsourcing identifies NPT and Renaissance Charitable Foundation as leading private-label DAF sponsor choices, but does not identify NPT as a Ren customer. Both NPT and Ren appear among financial supporters of the 2025 Annual DAF Report; this is a shared research sponsorship, not a commercial agreement.

## Separate the claims
- **NPT's GivingPoint donor-facing UX built by Ren:** unverified.
- **Underlying NPT ledger/accounting/administration software built or supplied by Ren:** unverified; plausible alternative to investigate.
- **Earlier NPT portal before GivingPoint provided by Ren/Crown/DonorFirst:** unverified.
- **NPT private-label partners using Ren separately:** unverified; could be distinct sponsor and channel arrangements.
- **NPT and Ren have industry/research links:** documented, not proof of software development.

## Competing hypotheses
H1: Ren developed GivingPoint front-end and NPT branded it. H2: Ren supplied a backend integrated with an NPT-developed or third-party front-end. H3: Ren supplied a predecessor platform that was replaced in 2021. H4: NPT built or commissioned an independent system and no Ren platform relationship exists. H5: Ren relationship applies only to a specific institutional/private-label program, not all NPT accounts.

## Tests
1. Locate archived 2018–2022 NPT portal domains, source/attribution, procurement announcements, software project case studies and vendor announcements.
2. Search 990 Schedule O and Part IX for Ren/Renaissance/Crown and software supplier payments, recognizing that supplier names may be absent from public returns.
3. Review authorized NPT GivingPoint portal documentation and publicly accessible network/domain metadata; do not infer vendor from shared technologies or unauthorized access.
4. Compare NPT and Ren platform user flows, naming and software releases without claiming common code from visual similarity.
5. Request confirmation from NPT or Ren of developer, operator, platform lineage, modules, and current service arrangement.
6. Establish whether the historical relationship predates Ren's 2016 Crown acquisition, 2021 Pinkaloo acquisition or 2023 iPhi acquisition.
