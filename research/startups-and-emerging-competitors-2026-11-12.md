# Startup and Small-Organization DAF Competitive Research
Date: 2026-11-12
Status: preliminary desk research. Company-reported metrics are not audited conclusions. Scope includes direct DAF sponsors, infrastructure vendors and adjacent entrants; distinguish them.

## Priority candidates and evidence

### Daffy — consumer DAF sponsor/platform (P0)
Reported $2B charitable assets in September 2026 (up from $720M in 2025), >$350M donation recommendations in first eight months 2026. Company calls figures unaudited. Membership tiers as of Aug 2026: free under $100; $3/$5/$20/$40 monthly tiers depending on account and contribution/features. Questions: unit economics and cross-subsidies, acquisition, donor retention, stock/private stock processing, costs and disbursement dependence, sustainability of flat fees.
- https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20260930616360/daffy-surpasses-2-billion-in-charitable-assets-5-years-after-launch
- https://help.daffy.org/en/articles/6323771-what-are-daffy-s-membership-fees

### Chariot — DAF payment and disbursement infrastructure (P0)
Claims 70+ fundraising-platform partners and $200M+ added to nonprofit fundraising. Does not compete primarily as sponsor. Test network effects, processing economics, revenue model, partner concentration, error rates, dependence on sponsors, potential commoditization.
- https://www.givechariot.com/about
- https://www.dafday.com/about

### Charityvest / Foundation Source — independent sponsor plus acquired technology vendor (P0)
Foundation Source acquired Vennfi (technology powering Charityvest) in Oct 2024, not Charityvest's charitable assets. Charityvest is independently governed sponsor, Foundation Source is technology/services provider. Charityvest reported 2025 contributions $265.5M, grants $119.2M, 48,744 grants, $224.6M AUM. Charityvest announced Chariot disbursement transition starting Aug 2026. Study acquisition rationale, post-acquisition product evolution, institutional distribution and layered dependencies.
- https://foundationsource.com/newsroom/press-releases/foundation-source-acquires-vennfi-the-fintech-powering-donor-advised-fund-charityvest/
- https://guide.charityvest.org/about-our-venture/charityvest-%2B-vennfi
- https://foundationsource.com/newsroom/press-releases/charityvest-reports-record-2025-charitable-giving-more-than-doubling-contributions-and-grants/
- https://www.givechariot.com/resources/insights/getting-to-know-charityvest-a-new-chariot-disbursements-partner

### Groundswell — corporate giving and DAF technology (P1)
Claims unified giving/volunteering/grantmaking, GuideStar integration, grant distributions within 24 hours. Verify scope, time definitions, fees, sponsor relationships, SLA evidence and true operational exceptions.
- https://www.groundswell.io/products/donor-advised-fund

### GiveClear Foundation / TIFIN Give — sponsor plus platform distribution (P0)
GiveClear lists TIFIN Give, Blue Trust, Ingalls & Snyder, Arlington, Zions Bank partnerships; planned direct-consumer offering. SEI announced SEI Charitable Fund Oct 28 2026, powered by TIFIN Give and sponsored by GiveClear. Study control of donor/advisor relationships, contracts, data portability, unit economics and concentration risk.
- https://www.giveclear.org/reports-and-financials
- https://www.seic.com/about-sei/newsroom/sei-launches-sei-charitable-fund-bringing-charitable-giving-advisor-and-client-wealth-experience

### GivingIQ (formerly DonateStock) — noncash giving and DAF-adjacent infrastructure (P1)
April 2026 expansion to DAF, cryptocurrency and QCD gifts. Test nonprofit acquisition, disbursement integrations and whether broader asset intake creates defensibility.
- https://givingiq.com/press/announce

### Further discovery (P2, not established direct DAF competitors)
Founders Pledge; smaller specialist DAF sponsors; workplace giving startups; Givefront (nonprofit banking/finance adjacency). Verify product and organizational status before categorizing.
- https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/18/these-21-year-old-dropouts-raised-2m-to-build-givefront-a-fintech-for-nonprofits/

## Cross-company research questions
1. Sponsor vs technology vs custodian vs distribution vs payment: who owns what?
2. Is distribution through wealth advisors cheaper than direct donor acquisition?
3. Does flat-fee pricing sustain small-account administration?
4. How much operational work remains manual after modern UI and automation?
5. Does external disbursement infrastructure create network effects or dependency?
6. What do acquisitions, failures and abandoned products reveal?
7. How do new entrants handle repeat grants, risk and exception workflows?
8. Can nonprofits retain donor identity and relationship through DAF payment rails?
9. Which vendor claims have independent evidence, and which are marketing only?
10. What is the cost of data portability and changing sponsors/providers?

## Clarity / EDF protocol
Record each claim, dated source, entity boundary, measurement definition, mechanism, counterevidence, alternative explanation, confidence and implication. Keep marketing metrics labeled; normalize periods and distinguish contributions, assets, grants and transactions.
