# DAF and philanthropy professional education: AI coverage (2026-11-21)

**Scope.** This file covers the education ecosystem *around* the DAFCP. DAFCP, CAP, CFP, CFRE, CSPG, CPWA/CIMA and sponsor 1-hour CE are already covered in `research/education/dafcp-syllabus-findings.md` and `research/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/05-credential-comparison.md`. They are not repeated here, except where a new fact bears on them.

**Tags.**
- **[F]**: read on the publisher's page or in its PDF on 2026-11-21.
- **[R]**: seen only in a search-engine snippet or summary. It must be confirmed before it is quoted.
- **[I]**: my inference.

**Rules followed.** No logins, paywalls or challenges were bypassed. There was no outreach. No LinkedIn was used.

**Method limits.**
- Many association pages (AFP, CGP, NTEN, AiP, Advisors in Philanthropy hub, Candid Learning) were not reached directly. Their entries rest on search summaries [R].
- "Not found" means not found in about 60 searches and fetches. It does not mean the thing is absent.
- Costs and hours are given only where a source stated them.

**Pattern [I].**
- Use is near-universal but shallow (drafting, notes, communications).
- Governance lags: policies at 30–40%, roadmaps at about 8%.
- Funders largely do not use AI for grant screening or verification. That is the back-office function most relevant to DAF sponsors.
- No survey found measures AI use **at DAF sponsors** specifically.

## (d) Textbook findings: Ted Hart, *The DAF Revolution*

1. **Bibliographic details.**
   - *The DAF Revolution: Making a Difference in Our Modern World*, Hart Philanthropy Press (AbeBooks lists the publisher as "Theodore R. Hart"), 3 Sep 2025, 328 pp., hardcover. [F P45, P46]
   - Hardcover ISBN **979-8-9997607-0-8**. The DAFCP syllabus ISBN 979-8-9997607-7-7 could not be matched in any retailer or search result. It is probably another format of the same book [I]. Treat the two ISBNs as distinct editions until confirmed.
   - The book is print-on-demand [R].
2. **Publisher description.**
   - It draws on "more than 120 experts".
   - Its themes are donor empowerment, advisor strategies, nonprofit readiness, global giving (100+ countries), **technology** and future policy.
   - The technology theme reads: **"how AI, blockchain, and digital platforms are expanding access and transparency."** [F P45, P46]
   - So AI is framed as a tool for access and transparency. There is no sign of operations, compliance or governance content [I].
3. **Table of contents.** **None public.** None was found on Schweitzer, AbeBooks, retailer listings or in search, and there is no publisher or book website [F P45, P46; R].
4. **Endorsements and awards.**
   - Rebecca Moffett, President of **Vanguard Charitable** [F P46].
   - Joe Fisher, President & CEO of **Ren**: "Ted does not shy away from tough questions" [F P46]. Fisher also **wrote the Foreword** [F P47].
   - Awards: 2026 Axiom Business Book Awards Bronze; 2026 Best Book Awards finalist [F P46].
   - No independent critical review was found.
5. **Author podcast ("Nonprofit Coach: and Everything DAF!", hosted by Hart)** [F P48]. Guests skew to sponsors and vendors:
   - Joe Fisher (Ren; launch-day episode, 3 Sep 2025);
   - Adam Nash (Daffy);
   - Rebecca Moffett (VC);
   - Russell James;
   - Allison Fine (Every.org; **"How AI Can Help Philanthropy Stay Human"**, 29 Oct 2025: "AI for empathy", automation that frees time for relationships) [F P49];
   - Amir Pasic;
   - Fred Kaynor;
   - Donna Callejon (17 Dec 2025: "technology's promise and its limits", sceptical of "AI-curated giving") [F P48];
   - Matt Nash (**Blackbaud Giving Fund**; "Technology, Trust, and DAFs": workplace giving, digital wallets; **no AI**) [F P50].
6. **Other interviews.** Nonprofit Counsel podcast (May Harris), 20 Jan 2026, on the book: DAF basics, criticisms, fundraising [R P51]. No AI content was stated.
7. **Implication [I].**
   - The required DAFCP text treats AI as a promotional or access theme, judging by its description.
   - The author's AI discussion is relational and donor-facing. It is not about the operational, compliance or governance side.
   - Sponsor and vendor voices (Ren, VC, Blackbaud Giving Fund, Daffy, Every.org) dominate the book's surrounding discussion.
