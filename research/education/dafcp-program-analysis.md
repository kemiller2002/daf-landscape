## 3. Updated findings from the education addendum

1. **The textbook links the credential to two more sponsor-side leaders.** The book named as the textbook was written by a DAFCP contributor (a former CAF America CEO). Its listing carries endorsements from Vanguard Charitable's president and Ren's CEO (register §2).
   - This revises the earlier "none found" result for Ren to **informal-professional (conditional)**.
   - It adds a further endorsement-level tie to Vanguard Charitable.
   - It is not evidence of any Ren or Vanguard Charitable role in the course.
2. **The required reading mentions AI.** Its description lists "AI, blockchain, and digital platforms" as a theme.
   - In public materials, AI moves from absent to **mentioned in the required-reading description**.
   - Programme materials themselves still do not mention AI [DE057].
   - See [ai-readiness-of-daf-education.md](ai-readiness-of-daf-education.md).
3. **A syllabus exists.** That is the most important unread source. Until the owner retrieves it (OF-525), any statement that a topic is absent from the course stays a statement about public outlines only.
