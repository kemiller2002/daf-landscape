# Owner decision: robots.txt no longer limits owner-directed research (2026-11-20)

**Decision.** On 2026-11-20 the repository owner directed:

> "If it's available then it's in scope for us. You're doing it for me so it's the same as a human doing it."

**Effect.**
- From this date, publicly available pages and documents that robots.txt disallows **may be fetched** when the research is directed by the owner. This mirrors the owner's earlier lifting of the site-terms restriction ([FINAL-STATUS](../mission-2026-11-14/FINAL-STATUS.md), PR #74).
- Requests stay at human-browsing volume.

**Still not allowed:**
- bypassing logins, CAPTCHAs, bot challenges, paywalls or other access controls;
- submitting forms;
- contacting anyone or representing the owner externally without authorisation;
- reproducing copyrighted course or textbook content beyond short quotes.

**Earlier provenance notes stay as written.** Batches before this date record which sources were skipped because of robots.txt. Those notes remain historically accurate. Sources previously blocked only by robots.txt can now be re-read. The [owner-fetch target list](../../data/mission-2026-11-14/owner-fetch-targets-2026-11-20.csv) is a backlog of such sources. Rows whose block reason is `robots_*` can now be fetched directly, while rows blocked by challenges or logins still need the owner.

**First use (2026-11-20).** These documents are linked from the College's public syllabi guide (`theamericancollege.libguides.com/syllabi`). Content is quoted only briefly; the PDFs are not stored in the repository.
