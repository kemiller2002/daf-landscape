## 3. Corrections to earlier files (governing)

1. **The textbook ISBN in the brief was correct**, and its required status is verified ([study-source register](dafcp-study-source-register.md) §2; [programme analysis](dafcp-program-analysis.md) §1, §3).
2. **The Ren relationship is verified, not conditional.** Ren's CEO endorses the required text written by a DAFCP contributor. This is informal-professional. It applies to [04 relationship map](../dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/04-industry-relationships-and-influence-map.md) row "Ren" and to [relationship-map.csv](../../data/dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/relationship-map.csv) RM20.
3. **Curriculum reconstruction** ([dafcp-public-curriculum.md](dafcp-public-curriculum.md)):
   - D: "Compliance and approval workflows" and grant-administration practices change from implied to **explicit** (CLO5).
   - D: "Investment management" now explicitly includes **impact investing**.
   - F: "Fiduciary concerns" changes from implied to **explicit** (CLO4, sponsor fiduciary standards).
   - The technology row stays "explicit (generic)": responsible tool use. AI remains absent as a curriculum topic; it appears only as a student-conduct policy.
4. **Exam specification** ([05 credential comparison](../dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/05-credential-comparison.md)): the DAFCP exam is 100 questions in 150 minutes, closed book, proctored, one attempt, no retakes.
5. **Faculty role** ([04 map](../dafcp-investigation-2026-11-20/04-industry-relationships-and-influence-map.md) §3): the named experts developed content and deliver lectures (CLO materials text). "Named contributor" therefore understates their role.
6. **New commercial tie:** the CAP 549 required text is published by Charitable Solutions LLC (DE080).
7. **Research-instrument limit** ([certification note](../commercial-opportunity/certification/dafcp-commercial-value.md)): an enrolled researcher may not share course content with non-enrolled people or with AI platforms. Research output must therefore be the researcher's own analysis, not reproduced or AI-processed course content.
