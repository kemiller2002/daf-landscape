## Definition of done
A provider is **not complete** until evidence distinguishes (1) public eligibility rule, (2) sponsor policy, (3) product UI/workflow, (4) software automation vs human reviewer, (5) actual performance and exceptions, (6) grant funding, (7) payment/recipient acknowledgment, (8) appeal and oversight, and (9) limitations. Each conclusion needs contrary evidence, as-of date, source and confidence. Never claim unseen internal workflows.
