For Nonprofits
Get grants faster and more safely with EFT
When you sign your nonprofit up for Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) from Fidelity Charitable, grants will be directly deposited in your nonprofit's bank account. Fidelity Charitable offers both Stripe and ACH as options for EFT.
Benefits of receiving grants from EFT:
Our EFT program is free for your organization
Receive grants up to 9x faster than with paper checks
With 1 in 5 organizations reporting mail fraud, EFT helps protect grants recommended by your donors
Enroll through Stripe
Enroll through ACH
Please note, the ACH queue may take up to 4 weeks. We recommend Stripe for faster EFT enrollment.
