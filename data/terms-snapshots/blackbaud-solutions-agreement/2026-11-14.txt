Documentation for Your internal business purposes during the term set forth in the Order Form. Solutions may only be
used or accessed from Your devices and systems that meet the System Requirements.

reinstate the affected Solutions as quickly as possible.

a. We have implemented and will maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to: (i) protect against
anticipated threats or hazards to the security of Your Confidential Information; (ii) protect against unauthorized access to

Security Breach to You.

responsible for Your Users' compliance with this Agreement.

15. Term And Termination
a. Term. The term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated pursuant to this
Section 15 or until the expiration of the current term if cancelled in accordance with an Order Form.
b. Material Breach. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach of its
obligations under this Agreement and the material breach remains uncured for at least thirty (30) days following receipt
of written notice from the party requesting termination, or by Blackbaud upon ten (10) days' written notice for Your
failure to pay undisputed invoices when due. In addition, this Agreement may be terminated by either party upon
written notice if the other party makes an assignment for the benefit of its creditors or dissolves its entity.
c. Effect of Termination. Upon termination of this agreement or termination of a Solution or Service, Blackbaud may
immediately cease providing the terminated Solution(s) and/or Service(s). All earned and unpaid fees and expenses are
due upon termination.
d. Return/Destruction of Your Data. After the effective date of expiration or termination ("Termination Date"), You will no
longer have a right to access Your Data within the Solution or to receive a copy of Your Data. Blackbaud will, within a
reasonable time after the Termination Date and consistent with applicable data privacy laws, destroy Your Data. In order
to receive a copy of Your Data before it is destroyed, You must extract Your Data from the Solution while you have
access, or submit a request to Blackbaud Support a minimum of thirty (30) days prior to the Termination Date to allow
Support sufficient time to fulfill the request. Upon receipt of such a timely request, Blackbaud Support, at Blackbaud's
option, will either provide guidance to You in extracting Your Data, or where applicable, a backup copy. If Blackbaud
creates backup copies of Your Data during the course of performing its obligations under this Agreement, You agree
that Blackbaud may retain such copies following termination until destroyed pursuant to Blackbaud's internal practices
for record destruction, which data will be subject to the provisions of this Agreement until destroyed.
